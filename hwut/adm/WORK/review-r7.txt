THE GENERAL LESSON. A citation by PATH breaks when the path moves; a
citation by RULING NUMBER does not. Every reference above named a file;
none named an E-/R-/O-/D- entry. Where the thing cited is settled, the
number is the better citation and the path is the worse one.

3  DOUBLE ENTRIES

3.1  THREE FILES SHARE ONE TITLE -- and it is NOT duplication.

    07  scheduler   disc-1  PROVISIONAL CHOICES, TAKEN TO BUILD...
    09  run         disc-1  PROVISIONAL CHOICES, TAKEN TO BUILD...
    0f  plan        disc-3  PROVISIONAL CHOICES, TAKEN TO BUILD...

    Read together, they are three DIFFERENT lists -- one per component,
    each about that component's own provisional decisions. The title is
    a genre, not a subject.

    BUT they are entangled: 0f's items 3, 5 and 9 are STUBS reading
    'MOVED to orchestrator/scheduler/DISCUSSIONS', and their contents
    are 07's items 1, 2 and 3. So the same three decisions were
    recorded twice -- once as content, once as a pointer. The pointers
    now name the gathered file and its item number.

3.2  TWO NUMBER COLLISIONS INSIDE ONE COMPONENT.

        0w  todo-1  THE 'output' PARAMETER -- SUBJECTS ARE THE TEST'S

    is one of the things the id scheme is for.

    Operations carries the same defect independently: TWO todo-23s
