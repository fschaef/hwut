INTENT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE SAME BRANCH RECURS. Measured on 1b5288c, before the demo leaves went
(T-j, T-k): the TEST HEADERS of all 17 ports linked the SAME 55 names --
not one port more, not one less (CORRECTED 2026-11-09: the earlier "16
ports 12, python 59" counted the registry's DEFINITIONS, which the
ports' own feature files stated unevenly; the links never differed).
'demo-passes-on-every-run' is one of the 55; 54 name what a demo shows.
Collapsed to one feature per port, 'demo-<language>' says that a
demonstration works, and no longer WHICH of hwut's features it
demonstrates.

A PROTOTYPE says it once. It is a branch that is no feature of its own;
a feature AGGREGATES it (has-a), and so has its members as children of
its own -- each member a feature of THAT owner, proven by THAT owner's
runs.

    +demo-compare : cover-exact, cover-numeric-ratio, ..., +demo-pype
    +demo-pype    : cover-order-law, cover-terminal-token
    demo-rust     : +demo-compare
    demo-python   : +demo-compare, fsm-law-kept, fsm-law-broken, ...

    demo-rust
      demo-rust+demo-compare
        demo-rust+cover-exact               <- test-*.rs links this
        demo-rust+cover-numeric-ratio
        demo-rust+demo-pype
          demo-rust+cover-order-law
          demo-rust+cover-terminal-token

T-j stands: 'numeric-ratio' is engine/compare's promise. 'demo-rust+
cover-numeric-ratio' is the port's: that its demonstration shows it.
T-k is refined, not overturned: a port promises demonstrations only --
one per thing it demonstrates instead of one in all.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RULED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
P-a  A PROTOTYPE IS AGGREGATED, A HAS-A RELATION (2026-11-09). A feature
     may have prototypes and children of its own side by side: python
     has the general demo prototype PLUS its own.

P-b  A PROTOTYPE MAY AGGREGATE A PROTOTYPE (2026-11-09).

P-c  NO CONCATENATION OF PROTOTYPE IDS (2026-11-09): a combination of
     prototypes is itself a prototype aggregating them. Hence every
     composed name has exactly two parts (p-1).

P-d  THE PROTOTYPE IS DECLARED IN THE FOREST (2026-11-09, fork 1 (a)):
     a line whose left side begins with '+' declares a prototype; a
     right-side entry '+<id>' aggregates one. One file, one sign, read
     the same at the declaration and at the use. T-a's three files
     stand.

P-e  PYTHON'S OWN FEATURES ARE PLAIN CHILDREN of 'demo-python' beside
     '+demo-compare' (2026-11-09).

P-f  'f/+<prototype>' IS REFUSED (2026-11-09, o-1): it refers to no
     instance. A GLOB OVER THE OWNERS MAY STAND: 'f/*+<prototype>', the
     runs of every instance. This amends E-214 / t-8, which refused every
     glob in 'f/' -- see o-3 for how far it reaches.

P-g  ANY GLOB, IN ANY POSITION OF 'f/' (2026-11-09, o-3 (b)):
     'f/demo-*+cover-*', 'f/cover-*', 'f/*'. E-214's refusal of a glob,
     and t-8's "a glob is no id", are OVERTURNED for 'f/'; a plain word
     without a glob character stays one id, refused by name where it
     stands nowhere.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONSEQUENCES READ OUT OF THE RULINGS -- strike what is over-read
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
p-1  TWO-PART NAMES. A composed name is '<feature>+<id>', '<id>' a
     prototype or a member anywhere below the aggregated prototype. The
     place comes from the prototypes, the name from the owner and the
     id. '+' lies outside the id alphabet (T-i): no written id collides
     with a composed one.

p-2  ONE NAME, ONE PLACE. An owner reaching the same prototype or member
     twice -- directly and through another prototype -- is a FAULT (the
     one-parent rule of T-o, one level up). So is a circle among
     prototypes. Both checked where the forest is read (t-3).

p-3  EVERY MEMBER AND EVERY PROTOTYPE STANDS UNDER ONE PROTOTYPE across
     all of them, so '<id>' alone says which is meant.

p-4  A PROTOTYPE IS NO FEATURE: no runs, no state, no count, never a
     root, never unplaced. An instance and its members fold as features
     do (spec 43 section 4). A member no run of the owner links is OPEN
     -- the honest word for "this port does not demonstrate it".

p-5  THE DOC. '[[cover-exact]]' documents the member in every instance;
     '[[demo-rust+cover-exact]]' may stand and wins for that one. A
     prototype's own block, '[[+demo-compare]]', documents its
     instances. Audiences fold down the instance tree as down any other.

p-6  THE LINKS name the composed form, 'features = ["demo-rust+cover-
     exact"]'; so does 'f/demo-rust+cover-exact'. A link to a bare member
     ('cover-exact') stands outside the relation: a member is proven only
     as an owner's.

p-7  RENAME AND RETIRE of a member or a prototype act on every instance
     and every link in one step.

p-9  A GLOB THAT MATCHES NO FEATURE IS REFUSED, by name: the reason
     E-214 had for refusing globs -- a misspelt selection that silently
     selects nothing -- is kept where it bites. Not strict (a derived
     label of 'hwut-root.labels'), it selects nothing, as an unknown id
     does. A glob matches composed names and plain ids alike, never a
     prototype itself (P-f): 'f/*' is every feature, 'f/+*' is refused.

p-8  THE DETECTOR NOTES a prototype nobody aggregates, and a member
     without a block in a prototype without a block.
