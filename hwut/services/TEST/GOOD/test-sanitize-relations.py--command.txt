
--- relate: a moved directory ---
hwut.sanitize relate engine/plain/core

        engine/compare: childs, 'core' taken out
        engine/plain: childs, 'core' entered, carried with its key
status: OK

--- relate: again ---
hwut.sanitize relate engine/plain/core
    nothing to do: relate engine/plain/core -- the files and the map agree with the directories
status: OK

--- relate: the features it holds ---
hwut.sanitize relate engine/plain/core/TEST
    done: relate engine/plain/core/TEST
        map: 'engine.cmp.core.line-pairing' now means engine.plain.core.line-pairing
        map: 'engine.cmp.core.missing-side' now means engine.plain.core.missing-side
status: OK

--- relate: no such directory ---
hwut.sanitize relate engine/nowhere
    nothing to do: relate engine/nowhere -- nothing stands there
status: OK
