==============================================================================
HOWTO-COMPLETION -- TAB completes hwut's faces, options and words
==============================================================================

ONCE, after installing or after options changed:

    hwut --command-line-setup

asks every face for its options and writes them as one compiled table
beside the completer. Then, by the shell -- the lines it prints name
the paths:

    bash        in '~/.bashrc'     source <hwut>/services/shell/hwut.bash
    zsh         in '~/.zshrc',     source <hwut>/services/shell/hwut.zsh
                after 'compinit'
    PowerShell  in '$PROFILE'      . <hwut>/services/shell/hwut.ps1
 Every 'hwut' and 'hwut.*' command on
PATH completes from then on:

    hwut.run --f<TAB>                  --fail  --faster-than ...
    hwut.run --strategy <TAB>          the words the option takes
    hwut.run.play <TAB>                the test applications here
    hwut.run.play test-a.sh <TAB>      that application's choices
    hwut.run test-a.sh <TAB>           the same: the short form
    hwut.run -l <TAB>                  the labels of 'hwut-root.labels'
    hwut.execute <TAB>                 the targets this 'hwut.conf' binds
    hwut.run --dir <TAB>               directories

A TAB never runs a test, writes nothing, and is answered in about 30
milliseconds; where an answer would cost more, it offers nothing.

A face that reads its words by hand (TODO F-4) completes its name, not
yet its options.
