The rulings of adm/WORK/intend/42-platforms.txt (1-22) enter here with

PL-1  A RELATIVE 'recipe' IS MADE ABSOLUTE BY THE DECISION, NOT HERE
      (P-1, amended P-2, 2026-11-02; PROVISIONAL, put to the author)

PROBLEM   spec/42 section 1: 'recipe' is relative to the conf's
          directory. 'is_available(name, spec)' and 'start(name, spec,
          write)' are given no directory; neither is the conf's reader,
          which knows the conf only by the name it shows.

DECISION  The reader keeps 'recipe' as written. The decision that
          decides a bubble ('engine/orchestrator/plan/bubble.py', P-4)
          knows the bubble's directory and hands the helper a 'recipe'
          that opens from anywhere. The helper takes
          the path as given; what it SAYS is the path as the tree names
          it ('adm/container/Containerfile', relative to the tree's
          top).

PL-2  THE KIND CHECKS ITS OWN KEYS: 'check(name, spec)'
      (P-2, 2026-11-02)

PROBLEM   Ruling 14: hwut's schema reads 'name' and 'kind', every other
          key is the kind's. 'keys(kind)' answers which keys a kind
          knows, but not which it needs ('image') nor which values it
          takes ('engine').

DECISION  Each kind answers 'check(name, spec)': (key, text) per fault
          of its own keys. The conf's reader asks it after 'name' and
          'kind' read, and places each fault at its key. A later kind
          (HIL) brings its own 'check'; the reader is not touched.
