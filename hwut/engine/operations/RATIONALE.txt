R-70  THE TOKEN IS THE STREAM'S OWN TESTIMONY OF COMPLETENESS
                                                              (2026-09-04)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROBLEM   A subject stream can end for reasons that have nothing to do
          with the subject finishing: a cap blew, a process was
          killed, a crash landed mid-write, a run was cut short. The
          bytes that arrived are indistinguishable, on inspection,
          from the bytes of a stream that ran to its end. Every
          consumer downstream then reasons on evidence whose
          completeness it cannot establish -- acceptance would bless a
          truncated stream as the pole every later run is judged
          against; coverage would harvest lines from a process that
          testified to nothing; a comparison would report a wall of
          line differences where the honest reading is 'this stream
          never finished'.

          Nothing outside the stream can settle this. Exit codes speak
          for the process, not the output; wall-clock and byte counts
          are circumstantial. The completeness of a stream is a fact
          only the thing that ends the stream can state.

DECISION  A SUBJECT STREAM STATES ITS OWN COMPLETENESS. Its last
          non-empty line is the terminal token

              <hwut-end>

          The token is CONTENT -- compared, stored and displayed like
          any other line; the compare framework knows no plumbing.
          'consume/terminal.py' holds the word and the reading law.

          EVERY STDOUT NOMINAL ENDS IN THE TOKEN. RULED (Frank-Rene,
          2026-10-01), of the GOODs without it: show ". . . no
          <hwut-end> marker [FAIL]". A stdout nominal without the
          token is the INCOMPLETE RECORD: the case fails
          'nominal-without-hwut-end', worded 'no <hwut-end> marker'
          before '[FAIL]' (display D-31), whatever the subject says --
          equal is not complete. Its subject is then not judged.

          ABSENCE HAS ITS OWN NAME. A subject whose nominal carries
          the token and which does not draws
          'terminated-without-hwut-end' ('cut-short'). Both stand in
          the report precedence ABOVE the judgement, the nominal's
          first, so an incomplete stream never masquerades as an
          ordinary mismatch.

          WHO ENDS A STREAM ends it:
              plain        the application; HwutRunner emits the token
                           as the choice's last act
              interactive  the application, per choice, into the
                           stdout sink before 'done' -- each choice's
                           stream self-delimits, in band
              pype-d       THE PYPE SCRIPT, from its '<eof>' handler;
                           the application MUST NOT emit it

          The pype case is not a convenience. An application feeding a
          pype cannot place the token safely: subject to buffering,
          threads or races, it would be testifying to a completeness
          it is not in a position to observe. The pype's '<eof>'
          firing is the one honest proof that the pipe ran dry. So a
          pype-d stream that ends without the token is THE PYPE
          SCRIPT'S FAULT, and the diagnosis names the script, never
          the application. The framework enforces the application's
          side for the reference runner through 'HWUT_NO_TERMINAL'.

          FILE SUBJECTS ARE EXEMPT: their completeness is the READING
          POINT (after the token), not a token of their own.

COST      Every fixture and every hand-run application must speak the
          law and print the token as its last line -- the suites' own
          bash fixtures included, which were corrected rather than
          exempted. Every pype script gains an '<eof>' handler that
          prints it. A deliberate 'sys.exit(n)' from a pype block
          skips pending '<eof>' handlers and so forgoes the token; its
          nominal then lacks it too, and the case fails 'no <hwut-end>
          marker' until the script ends the stream.
          MEASURED (2026-10-01): 76 GOODs stood without the token, 74
          of them offered, on 21 pages of 'engine/compare' that never
          printed it; the pages were mended and the 74 re-accepted
          before the rule landed (the other two: an orphan and an
          application exploration does not reach).

GRAVEYARD An out-of-band completeness flag (a sidecar file, a field in
          the store): a second carrier of one truth, and one that a
          hand-run application would never write. Inferring
          completeness from the exit code: the code speaks for the
          process, and a process can exit 0 having printed half of
          what it meant to. Making the token a framework concern
          stripped before comparison: it would then be invisible in
          the GOOD file, and a reader could not see why a stream was
          refused.

CITED BY  E-6 and E-39 (services): acceptance refuses a candidate
          whose last line is not the token, and refuses evidence older
          than the test that produced it. The coverage harvest law
          (coverage/RATIONALE): "covered" means executed by a test
          that testified, and an ending without the token is not
          testimony.

O-35  AN EMPTY ANSWER SAYS WHY; THE CALL CARRIES ITS RESOLUTION     (2026-10-08)

PROBLEM   todo-21's first law -- an empty answer is never reported
          empty -- stood in 'diagnosis.py' and was wired to no face.
          MEASURED (audit r10, C-1): 'hwut.run.play' answered a page
          whose call could not start, or that said everything on
          stderr, with "EMPTY: the choice produced no output to read"
          and nothing else; its report helper read a field the
          delivery does not carry, so not even the token reached the
          screen. And the resolution chain was recomputed at render:
          honest for a live diagnosis, not for a recorded one.
DECISION  (1) THE CALL CARRIES ITS RESOLUTION: the execute stage takes
              'resolution_chain(argv[0])' when it makes the call, and
              the provision record ('Provision.resolution') carries it;
              'explain' shows the carried chain and recomputes only
              where nothing ran.
          (2) 'hwut.run.play' DIAGNOSES what answered nothing -- the
              EMPTY answer and the FAULT of a provision that delivered
              nothing: 'explain' beneath the word -- the call, what
              PATH resolved, every call's containment, exit and stderr
              tail, the report, the channels with the wrong-channel
              hint.
COST      A play's empty answer is now a dozen lines, not one.
OWED      The session-level switch that installs 'DiagnosisObserver'
          on 'hwut.run' (todo-21's "request flag"): not built; a run's
          empty case is diagnosed by playing it.
PINNED    'services/TEST/test-play.sh silent, why'.
