FOR THE ORCHESTRATOR -- what the two relations MEAN, so that nothing has to
be inferred from the keys:

    collision    a group of test runs -- a test application together with
                 its choices -- MAY NOT RUN IN PARALLEL. Every member of
                 a collision group runs alone with respect to the others.
                 What is named in no group may run in parallel with
                 anything.

    dependency   what it says: the named targets must have RUN before
                 this one starts. An ordering relation and not a success
                 relation (R-36) -- the verdict of a dependency does not
                 enter, and a dependant runs once its dependencies have
                 run to completion.

Exploration states the relations and settles satisfiability ([MISDEP],
R-35). HOW the orchestrator schedules what remains -- the order it picks
among the free ones, how many run at once -- is its own ruling, and
disc-1-test-sequence-form.txt is where that begins.
