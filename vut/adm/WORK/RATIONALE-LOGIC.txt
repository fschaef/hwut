==============================================================================
RATIONALE-LOGIC -- THE REASONING, GROUPED BY SUBJECT
==============================================================================

VERSION: 4

Goal (2b) of three. (2a) was the INDEX -- where every ruling stands.
This is the MAP -- what the rulings mean together.

WHAT THIS IS. Twelve subjects. Each names the rulings that constitute
it, IN READING ORDER (not number order), says what the subject settles,
and names the TENSIONS -- places where two rulings pull against each
other, or where one superseded another and both still stand.

WHAT IT IS NOT. Not a second copy of the reasoning. A ruling's words
stay in its own RATIONALE; this file is a route through them. Where it
disagrees with a RATIONALE, the RATIONALE is right.

WHY BY SUBJECT. The rulings cluster ACROSS components, not within them.
Identity runs through bookkeeper B-, orchestrator D-, coverage D- and
services E-. The token runs through operations R-70, services E-6/E-39
and coverage D-21. No component's RATIONALE shows a subject whole, and
reading one in isolation gives half a law.

it MIGRATION, and R-2 demotes its answers to HINTS. The seam is honest
only while both readings stay -- a hint that hardened into a source
would break R-7 silently.

==============================================================================

THE LAW IS LOCALITY, with two named exceptions. R-69 makes inheritance
local; R-73 lifts exactly one table to the root because per-language
tooling was being stated in four places; coverage D-2 lets the
component choose the tool while the conf names it.

THE TENSION IS RULED, AND WAS ALREADY (found 2026-09-04 by measuring,
not reading). gathered/0j asks whether 'target { }' inherits like R-73
or stays local, and calls it "the hardest fork of the cluster". E-7
answers it: "The scope is LOCAL, never inherited (R-69's locality law);
a parent cannot bind a target for a subtree." E-7 also settles the walk
question -- "a directory binding any target is WALKED, test
applications or none: definition is membership" -- and the service is
BUILT ('services/execute.py', 'bin/hwut.execute').

SO LOCALITY HAS EXACTLY ONE EXCEPTION, R-73's one table at the root,
and 'target { }' is not a second one. Subject 2 is settled throughout.

5  THE ORACLE: WHAT COUNTS AS EVIDENCE
==============================================================================
     R-70  E-6  E-39  D-21(cov)  E-5  E-41  O-18  O-14  D-10(disp)
     E-11  R-71

THE LAW. A run must TESTIFY. Output that arrives is not evidence that
the thing finished.

    R-70  (operations) THE TOKEN IS THE STREAM'S OWN TESTIMONY OF
          COMPLETENESS -- the spine
    E-6   accept refuses the stream that never completed
    E-39  a recording OLDER than its test is not evidence
    D-21  (coverage) coverage rides on testimony
    O-18  a failing canonicaliser ENDS provision -- it does not deliver
    E-5   stderr is NEVER subject to testing
    E-41  the gate is on the NOMINAL
    O-14  parallel-run suites bless the flow through a pype filter
    D-10  (display) the run's GOOD is a digest, and the digest is PART
          OF THE ORACLE
    E-11  the stain: instability is FALSE TESTIMONY, not a failure
    R-71  'output' -- subjects are DECLARED, never discovered

THE THREAD, AND IT IS ONE ARGUMENT. Only the thing that ends a stream
can say the stream ended (R-70). Everything else follows: acceptance
refuses an untestified stream (E-6) and stale evidence (E-39); coverage
refuses to harvest one (D-21); provision refuses to deliver a broken
canonicalisation (O-18); and instability is reclassified from FAILURE
to FALSE TESTIMONY (E-11), which is the same idea applied to a run that
cannot say the same thing twice.

E-41 IS THE SUBTLE ONE. Participation is decided by the NOMINAL, not by
a switch -- which is why R-70 needed no migration flag anywhere.

R-71 CLOSES IT. Discovery cannot tell 'produced' from 'forgot'; a
declared subject can.

THE THREAD. A tolerance is a named class of difference that is not a
difference (R-54). The rulings converge on ONE SCOPE (R-77, E-42) after
a period of flat parameters -- the same consolidation R-73 did for
language-setup.

THE FLOOR, RULED 2026-09-04 (adm/WORK/intend/16-pole-and-tolerance.txt):

        Eq( d(b_subject), d(b_nominal), t )    with    t( d(b_nominal) )

The tolerance is a function of THE NOMINAL'S DESCRIPTION. Nominals are
POLES, subjects are TRABANTS, and Eq is only ever called
subject-against-nominal. THE ASYMMETRY IS THE DOCTRINE. A symmetric
band would be worse than none: a subject drifting from the pole would
widen its own tolerance, and a centre whose scale is set by the thing
measured against it is not a centre.

'nominal.epsilon' in 'compare/reading/line_element.py' is therefore
CORRECT. todo-19's first defect (the missing 'abs()') was real and is
fixed; its second half is REFUSED by this ruling and the entry may be
struck whole.

OWED IN THE DOCUMENT, NOT THE CODE: PHILOSOPHY 1.1 still carries a
paragraph demanding a symmetric Eq and naming 'the larger magnitude' as
the remedy -- three paragraphs after the pole passage it contradicts.
It is STALE. Its durable half is the warning that follows it: "the
danger is not that the rule is missing; it is that NOTHING CHECKS IT,
and a tolerance can break it quietly."
