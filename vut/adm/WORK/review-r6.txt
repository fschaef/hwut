2  THE CAUSE, AND WHY IT IS NOT A DESIGN PROBLEM

'adm/bundle.sh' selects with 'git ls-files' by default -- TRACKED
FILES ONLY. '--complete'/-a adds 'git ls-files --others', i.e. the
untracked ones. A file created and not yet committed is therefore
SILENTLY ABSENT from a default-mode dump: no warning, no manifest
entry, nothing to notice.

The six modules are exactly what a fresh 'database/readers' split
would produce: new files, not yet committed. So

    coverage's todo-13, dated 2026-09-04, IS CORRECT AND CURRENT.
    It names 'coverage/database/TEST/' and 'coverage/readers/TEST/'
    because those directories EXIST in Frank-Rene's working tree.

r-5f is ANSWERED: coverage's prose is not describing a superseded
layout. The dump is describing an incomplete one.
