     The R- series is exploration's; R-70 is in operations, because the
     terminal token is content and operations owns the reading law. A
     signpost stands at exploration's R-69/R-71. Deliberate, not a hole.

     A FOURTH D- namespace, beside display, coverage and orchestrator.
     Nothing cites it from outside, so nothing is ambiguous today.

     LABELLED ENTRIES AT ALL -- bare PROBLEM/DECISION/COST/GRAVEYARD
     blocks, no handles. ITS RULINGS CANNOT BE CITED. It carries real
     doctrine: interactive mode as a pure ACCELERATOR (byte-exact with
     normal mode, one set of GOODs), and the file-name channel protocol.
     Both are load-bearing and neither has a number.

     A retirement recorded in the series itself -- the right way to
     leave a hole, and the only one in the tree that says so.

2.6  E-7's title begins lowercase ('hwut.execute -- ...') where every
     other heading is uppercase. Harmless, but it defeated a first
     extraction pass and would defeat a checker written the same way.
