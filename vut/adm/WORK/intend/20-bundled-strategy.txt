RULED (Frank-Rene): WE BUNDLE DIRECTORIES; SLOW DIRECTORIES FIRST; IN EACH
DIRECTORY, SLOW TEST RUNS FIRST; AN APPLICATION'S CHOICES TOGETHER.
This is cut C of three: the strategy member. Cut D (bundle an application's
choices at node level) and cut B ('--fastest') follow, each green first.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1  THE RULE, AND WHY IT IS NOT A NUMBER
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Open a further directory ONLY WHERE THE OPEN ONES CANNOT FILL THE BUDGET.

    free slots now            b
    admissible nodes the
    open units could start    r   (summed over open units)

    open the next unit  <=>  r < b

O-29's measurement is the evidence that a NUMBER is wrong: two directories
suffice at 8 jobs, three at 16, four at 16 for the last second -- the count
that serves is a function of the tree, not a constant. The rule asks the
units what they can do and opens exactly as far as it must.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2  WHAT THE TREE HOLDS TODAY  (measured, this tree)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
'CStrategy.may_start(index, running)'   sees the unit index and the set of
                                        units in flight. NOT the budget, NOT
                                        what the open units could start.
'CStrategy.run'                         wakes on FIRST_COMPLETED of a UNIT.
                                        A slot freed inside another directory
                                        wakes nothing here.
'Scheduler._dispatch_all'               already waits on 'budget.changed()'
                                        beside its own tasks, and asks
                                        'state.ready()' anew before every
                                        start (an exclusion set withdraws
                                        admission, so a snapshot lies).
'CDirectoryWork.run(emit, budget)'      builds its Scheduler INSIDE 'run';
                                        nothing outside can ask it anything.

So all three of the prompt's seams are real: strategy, CDirectoryWork,
Scheduler.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3  THE PLUMBING, SMALLEST FIRST
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  (1) Scheduler exposes 'ready_n()' -- how many nodes could start NOW, asked
      of 'state.ready()', never cached. Zero while it is blocked behind its
      own frame or an exclusion set. Its own '_dispatch_all' is untouched.
  (2) CDirectoryWork holds its Scheduler once built and answers 'ready_n()';
      zero before the Scheduler stands and after the unit ends.
  (3) CStrategy gains a BIND: the budget and the unit list, given once by
      CTreeScheduler. 'may_start' keeps its signature; BUNDLED reads what it
      needs off the binding. LINEAR, SUCCESSOR, PARALLEL ignore it.
  (4) CStrategy.run waits on 'budget.changed()' BESIDE the unit tasks where
      the strategy says it must ('wakes_on_budget_f'), and cancels the future
      as 'Scheduler._dispatch_all' does. Only BUNDLED sets it: the others'
      answers cannot change when a slot comes free.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4  WHAT IS OPEN, AND MUST BE RULED BEFORE THE DEFAULT MOVES
