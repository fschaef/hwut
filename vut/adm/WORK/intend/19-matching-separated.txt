o-2  THE GAP PENALTY, after 'WorkItemHistory' went -- NOT YET RULED.

     With it gone, a run of k missing lines costs k x 0.5, LINEAR. That
     is the flat end of the gap-penalty dial, and it says a removed
     block of forty lines is forty faults. The literature's middle
     position is affine -- a gap costs 'open + k x extend', which says
     a block is ONE fault and a bigger block is still a worse one --
     computed by Gotoh (1982) in O(mn) with three tables. The concave
     position, 0.5 * H(k), is what 'WorkItemHistory' intended; Waterman
     (1984) and Miller and Myers (1988) give algorithms for it.

     Anchoring may make this moot: within a five-line section the
     difference between linear, affine and concave is small. The
     position was left OPEN until anchoring is measured. ANCHORING IS
     BUILT AND MEASURED (C-15, C-16), so the measurement this fork
     waited for can now be made: the cost of a block deletion inside an
     anchored section under the three positions, on the fixture pair
     and on the real corpus. Nothing is built before that number
     stands ('adm/WORK/TODO.txt' band E).
