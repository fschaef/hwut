2.1  R-70 HAD NO RATIONALE ENTRY                            ** SETTLED **

    CORRECTED, version 2. Version 1 of this review said R-70 was
    'defined nowhere' and 'has no entry a reader can reach'. THAT WAS
    WRONG, and wrong in the direction that matters: the LAW was fully
    written; only the RATIONALE ENTRY was missing.

    exploration/RATIONALE.txt runs R-1 .. R-79 with exactly one hole
    at 70, and R-70 is cited four times as settled law:

        services/RATIONALE.txt:152   an application that does not
                                     speak R-70
        services/RATIONALE.txt:162   R-70 (the token is the stream's
                                     own ...)
        services/RATIONALE.txt:1455  the token (R-70)
        coverage/RATIONALE.txt:840   R-70: the token IS the statement

    But the law itself stands, in full, in TWO documents that already
    carry the number as a heading annotation:

        test_writing_support/hwut_pype/MANUAL.txt:84
            1b  THE TERMINAL TOKEN '<hwut-end>'  (R-70) -- READ THIS
        engine/operations/README.txt:1219
            THE TERMINAL TOKEN  (R-70)

    Frank-Rene's git search settled the history: the heading 'R-70'
    never stood in any blob on any branch. The number was allocated in
    conversation, cited immediately, and the entry was never written --
    while the MECHANICS were written twice, to a README and a MANUAL.

    RULED (2026-09-04), fork (a): the entry is WRITTEN, and it stands
    in 'engine/operations/RATIONALE.txt' -- a file that did not exist
    and was created for it. The token is CONTENT of a subject stream
    and 'operations/consume/terminal.py' holds the word and the
    reading law, so the entry stands with its owner. Exploration's
    hole at 70 is LEFT STANDING, with a signpost between R-69 and R-71
    naming where the law lives. A hole with a signpost is honest; a
    law filed under a component that does not own it is the drift this
    migration exists to stop.

    STILL OWED, as a consequence: both documents carry RATIONALE in
    the middle of mechanics -- the MANUAL's 'it cannot place the token
    safely -- an application subject to buffering, threads or races
    would lie about its own completeness', operations/README's 'the
    token is CONTENT ... the compare framework knows no plumbing'.
    That reasoning now stands in R-70 as well, so it is stated twice.
    Trimming the two documents back to mechanics is a separate edit,
    against user-facing manual text, and is NOT done here.
