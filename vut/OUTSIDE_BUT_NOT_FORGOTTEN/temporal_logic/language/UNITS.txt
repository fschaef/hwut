UNITS -- the physical unit glossary (R-50)
==========================================
The admitted unit NAMES. Every name expands to a rational exponent
vector over the seven SI base units; two spellings of one unit always
display identically (the canonical quotient print: positive powers left
of the bar, negative right, sorted; '1' the dimensionless nominator).

R-50 scopes DIMENSION, not scale: magnitude prefixes (k, m, c, ...) are
NOT modelled; 'g' aliases the kg dimension.

THE SEVEN SI BASES (display order)
----------------------------------
    kg      mass
    m       length
    s       time
    A       electric current
    K       thermodynamic temperature
    mol     amount of substance
    cd      luminous intensity

DERIVED (dimension expansions)
------------------------------
    g       kg                      (dimension alias; scale unmodelled)
    Hz      1/s
    N       kg*m/s^2
    Pa      kg/(m*s^2)
    J       kg*m^2/s^2
    W       kg*m^2/s^3
    C       A*s
    V       kg*m^2/(s^3*A)
    Ohm     kg*m^2/(s^3*A^2)
    F       s^4*A^2/(kg*m^2)
    T       kg/(s^2*A)
    Wb      kg*m^2/(s^2*A)
    lm      cd                      (the steradian is 1)
    lx      cd/m^2

The executable twin is language/units.py (GLOSSARY); a name admitted
there and not here -- or here and not there -- is a documentation
fault.
