THE FACES -- WHAT EACH DOES, AND WHERE THEY COINCIDE
______________________________________________________________________________

READ FROM THE CODE, not from the prose: an action counts where the
face CALLS it, so a docstring mentioning a thing does not vote. The
matrix below is what 'adm/import_graph.py' and a call scan agree on
(2026-08-29).

WHY IT EXISTS: a face's job is to parse, refuse, WIRE and report. What
several faces wire alike is a brick that either exists or is missing,
and this document is where that is decided by looking rather than by
argument.

==============================================================================
THE ACTIONS
==============================================================================
    SELECT     given a wish, which runs        'exploration/selection'
    WISH       read the command line's wish    'plan/wish'
    LABELS     open 'hwut-root.labels'         'services/labels'
    BOUNDARY   climb to 'hwut-root.conf'       'tree_explorer'
    BOOK       the record: name it, hold it    'bookkeeper'
    PLAN       nodes, links, exclusions        'plan/determine'
    RUN        build, launch, contain          'operations', 'run'
    COVER      coverage records                'coverage'
    FOLLOW     a changed name into records     'services/_follow'
    REPORT     fold the event stream           'protocol'
    RENDER     put a picture on a stream       'display', 'compare'
    WRITE      change what is stored

==============================================================================
WHAT EACH FACE DOES
==============================================================================
run          WISH, LABELS, RUN, REPORT, RENDER
             selects THROUGH the orchestrator, not itself -- the
             selection happens in 'determine_tree', one level down.
plan         SELECT, WISH, LABELS, PLAN
             takes the QUERY from the selection, because 'determine'
             reads a task list rather than a list of cases.
wishlist     SELECT, WISH, LABELS
             the selection, printed. Nothing else.
accept       SELECT, WISH, LABELS, BOOK, WRITE
             promotion: the candidate becomes the nominal.
report       SELECT, WISH, LABELS
             reads the book BEHIND the selection -- the Bookkeeper it
             uses is the selection's own.
stability    WISH, BOOK, REPORT
             runs the same wish repeatedly, through 'hwut.run'.
play         SELECT, WISH, RUN, RENDER
             one run, provisioned and read. Records nothing.
tell         BOUNDARY, BOOK, COVER
             packs what the stores hold, for a bug hunter.
cov          COVER
             coverage, as verbs.
show         LABELS, BOUNDARY, BOOK
             the configuration as the framework read it.
compare      RENDER          one comparison, rendered.
merge        (nothing shared) two nominals into one.
target       (nothing shared) a user-defined verb over the tree.
rename       BOOK, FOLLOW    a name changed, everywhere it is written.
remove       BOOK, FOLLOW, WRITE   a name forgotten.
rename-choice, remove-choice     shims onto the two above.

labels/create   WISH, LABELS, BOUNDARY, WRITE
labels/add      WISH, LABELS, BOUNDARY, WRITE
labels/remove   WISH, LABELS, BOUNDARY, WRITE
labels/list     LABELS, BOUNDARY
labels/query    LABELS, BOUNDARY, SELECT (all_of_tree)

==============================================================================
WHERE THEY COINCIDE -- WHAT THE COMPARISON FOUND
==============================================================================
1  THE SELECTION -- SETTLED (R-73)
   Seven faces assembled 'explore + Bookkeeper + query + get_test_cases'
   by hand. Now 'exploration/selection.py' does, and hands back what
   it built. Faces knowing 'Bookkeeper' fell from NINE to FOUR, and
   the four are the ones whose subject IS the record.

2  'WISH + LABELS + BOUNDARY' -- SETTLED for the three writers (E-24)
   Every face taking a wish also builds a label view, and nearly every
   one also climbs to the boundary. THE THREE LABEL WRITERS HAVE THE
   IDENTICAL ROW -- 'create', 'add' and 'remove' are one action with
   three verbs applied to the entry set.
   BUILT: 'labels/_editing.py' -- 'opened', 'stands_f', 'selected',
   'written'. Six imports fell out of the three faces.
   WHAT REMAINS OF THIS ROW: the other eight faces take a wish and
   build a label view, but each does something different with it. The
   coincidence there is 'view_at(directory)' and nothing more, which
   is already one line and already one place.

3  TWO WALKS, NOT ONE -- OPEN, AND THE INTERESTING ONE
   'run' does not select: 'determine_tree' selects for it, one level
   down. So the tree is walked and queried in TWO places -- once for
   faces ('selection.of_tree'), once for the runner. They share the
   walk, the Bookkeeper decision and the query; they differ in what
   they answer, cases against a plan.
   WHETHER THEY SHOULD BE ONE IS NOT OBVIOUS and is not settled here.

4  FACES THAT SHARE NOTHING -- WORTH A LOOK
   'merge' and 'target' call nothing that any other face calls. Either
   they are genuinely peripheral, or they have built private
   machinery. Not yet examined.

5  'show' TAKES 'TestIdDb' TO DISPLAY IDS
   The only BOOK reader whose subject is not the record. A read of the
   register, not bookkeeping -- small, and worth a name of its own if
   a second face ever wants it.
