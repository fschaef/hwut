P-2: one filler per subject line, and what the filler is
     0 subject line(s) -> ()
     1 subject line(s) -> ('##! unaccepted', '--?--', '####')
     3 subject line(s) -> ('##! unaccepted', '--?--', '--?--', '--?--', '####')

     filler = '--?--'
     It is significant CONTENT, so a filler against a subject
     line is a REAL difference -- which is what 'nobody has
     judged this' must look like. Anything of the '##'..'##'
     family would read as VISIBLE_NOTHING and the region would
     compare EQUIVALENT: a first acceptance that PASSES.
