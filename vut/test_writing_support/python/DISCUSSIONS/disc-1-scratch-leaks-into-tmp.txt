disc-1  SCRATCH DIRECTORIES LEAK INTO THE MACHINE'S /tmp      ** CLOSED **
______________________________________________________________________________

OBSERVED (2026-08-30): '/tmp' fills with 'vut_ids_*', 'vut_mutex_*',
'vut_multi_*', 'vut_store_*', 'vut_provider_*' -- 108 of them after
one afternoon's sweeps on the author's machine.

WHERE THEY COME FROM. 33 test applications call 'tempfile.mkdtemp'
(prefixes 'vut_prov_', 'vut_acc_', 'vut_eq_', 'vut_cov_', 'vut_walk_',
... and the five above). The removal is not guaranteed:

    test-directory-mutex.py     mkdtemp 1, rmtree 0
    test-provider.py            mkdtemp 1, rmtree 0
    test-stream_store.py        mkdtemp 1, rmtree 0
    test-multi-execute.py       mkdtemp 1, rmtree 1, no 'finally'
    test-test_id_db.py          rmtree at the END of each choice: a
                                choice killed by the supervisor (the
                                seven of disc-6 r-2) leaves every one

Two causes, then: tests that never remove, and tests that remove only
on the happy path -- and the interactive runner keeps the process
alive across choices, so an 'atexit' would fire only at the session's
end, and a kill never lets it fire at all.

THE FIX THAT WAS TRIED AND TAKEN BACK. Launch every test process with
'TMPDIR=<test dir>/TMP/scratch', so that whatever 'tempfile' makes
lands under the transient root and 'rm -rf TMP/' -- or 'hwut.sanitize
--transient' -- clears it. Built, run, reverted in the same pass:

  -  it is a BEHAVIOUR CHANGE, and bookkeeper todo-1 ruled pass A
     'no behaviour changes, wide, mechanical';
  -  a fixture tree made under 'TMP/scratch' stands INSIDE the
     project's tree, and its climb finds the project's own
     'hwut-root.conf' -- so 'test-tree.py root' ("RootConfMissing,
     as owed") stopped being owed, and every fixture reading that
     relied on standing outside any tree derived a language it never
     had. Fixtures that want a boundary already write one
     ('script_runner.tree_boundary'); fixtures that want NONE have no
     way to say so once '/tmp' is inside the tree.

RECOMMENDATION, two parts, one ruling:

  1  'script_runner.scratch(prefix)' -- ONE way to make a scratch
     directory in a test: registers the path with the reference runner,
     which removes every registered path when the CHOICE ends, happy
     path or exception, and at process exit for what is left. The 33
     call sites become one call each. A kill still leaks, and that is
     honest: a killed process cannot clean.

  2  The runner's scratch ground is '<test dir>/TMP/scratch' by
     DEFAULT, with 'scratch(prefix, outside=True)' for the handful of
     tests that must stand outside any tree ('test-tree.py root'). The
     leak of a killed process then lands where '--transient' clears it,
     and the machine's '/tmp' sees nothing of this project.

     Part 2 is the behaviour change; it is the runner's (test-writing
     support), not the framework's, and reaches the framework's own
     suites only. Ruled together with part 1 or after it.

CLOSED (2026-08-30, E-27, R-76). Part 2 was ruled and built: the
procsitter exports 'TEST/TMP/scratch/<call>' as TMPDIR and lists what
the call left ('created_tuple'); the climb ends at 'TEST/TMP', so a
fixture built there stands outside every tree. Part 1 -- one
'scratch()' in the runner that removes at the choice's end -- is
still worth having and is not built; it is now a tidiness, not a
leak.
