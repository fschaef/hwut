PROBLEM
-------
A GOOD file stores the verdict and not the grounds. Line 412 says
"berta". It does not say that "berta" is correct because the sort is
stable and Berta was inserted before Caesar.

Consequence: the authority behind the blessing is not transferable.
Years later a difference appears, the expert who blessed is gone, and
the reader holds a notarised document from a judge who cannot be
recalled to the stand. The only move left is re-blessing. This is the
failure mode that discredits golden-master testing elsewhere
(snapshot rot).

The free-description model already raises the quality of the expert's
judgement — a clear picture is easier to judge than an assert. What is
missing is the record of who judged, and on what grounds.

Fields that a blessing may carry:

  - warrant: the one sentence that made the behaviour correct
