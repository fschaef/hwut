NOTE — neighbouring tools, for context and for positioning
==========================================================

Written from recollection, not verified against current versions.
Verify before any of this is quoted publicly.

GOLDEN-MASTER FAMILY (same idea, different reach)
-------------------------------------------------
ApprovalTests        Preaching this model since roughly 2007. Multi-
                     language. Vocabulary: "approve" rather than
                     "bless". Diff-tool launch on mismatch.

Jest snapshots       JavaScript. Enormous installed base. Where the
                     term "snapshot rot" comes from: -u re-blesses
                     everything, and everything is what gets
                     re-blessed on a Friday.

insta                Rust. Well regarded. Has an interactive review
                     mode (accept / reject / skip, per file) that is
                     worth studying against the equivalence-class
                     re-blessing idea.

Consequence for positioning: free description of behaviour is not the
differentiator. That continent is settled. The differentiators are:

  - tolerant comparison (analogies, numeric tolerance, potpourri)
  - --hwut-info choice combinatorics
  - language independence at the process boundary
  - integrated coverage, with numbers that travel
    (binary .cover: 45x smaller than lcov, 30x faster to read than
     cobertura, measured on 1516 files / 140778 ranges)

"Snapshots, but ours" loses. "Behavioural description that survives
nondeterminism, across languages, with combinatorial sweeps and
coverage" is not sold by anyone.

PROPERTY-BASED FAMILY (complement, not rival)
---------------------------------------------
Hypothesis           Python. QuickCheck lineage (Haskell, 1999).

The idea: the framework generates inputs — many, and shrunk to the
smallest failing case when something breaks. The author does not
enumerate cases; the author states a property that must hold over all
of them.

The weak point of the family is the oracle. Stating a property strong
enough to be interesting is hard, and most users fall back to trivial
properties (does not crash, round-trips).

The pairing is unusually clean: Hypothesis owns input generation,
HWUT owns the oracle. Generated stimulus, painted reaction, blessed
picture. Nobody has shipped that combination.

Worth reading specifically: their shrinking, and their database of
previously failing examples (a persisted corpus of interesting inputs
— close in spirit to a roster).
