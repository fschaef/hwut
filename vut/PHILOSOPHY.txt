1  THE OBJECT
______________________________________________________________________________

Behaviour is what is judged. A green light is owed exactly when the
behaviour was admissible -- both directions of the "if and only if" carry
weight, and section 7 shows that they fail differently.

The behaviour belongs to a UNIT: a causal system with a causal contract and
a consistency contract. A TEST is one complete arc of that unit --
compilation, run, termination -- observed from outside the process. A CHOICE
is one scenario of that arc.


1.1  WHEN A TEST IS WORTH HAVING
______________________________________________________________________________

Write D for the transformation from BEHAVIOUR to DESCRIPTION -- what the
unit does, turned into the text a test reads. Write Eq(x, y, T) for the
comparison of two descriptions under a stated tolerance T. Let GOOD be
the admissible behaviours and BAD the inadmissible ones, and let

    GOOD  intersect  BAD  =  {}

THE CRITERION. A test, and the description it rests on, is worth having
IF AND ONLY IF

    for all b0, b1 in GOOD :        Eq(D(b0), D(b1), T)  =  true
    for all b0 in GOOD, b in BAD :  Eq(D(b0), D(b),  T)  =  false

That is: Eq o D collapses ALL of GOOD into ONE class, and NO member of
BAD falls into it.

                      D                      Eq(-, -, T)
    BEHAVIOUR  ------------->  DESCRIPTION  ------------->  one class

         .--------.                     .--------------.
         |  GOOD  | ------------------> |  ONE class   |   every good
         '--------'                     '--------------'   behaviour,
                                                           indistinguishable
         .--------.                     .--------------.
         |  BAD   | ------------------> | anywhere ELSE|   no bad behaviour,
         '--------'                     '--------------'   ever inside

TWO WAYS TO FAIL, and they are the only two:

    a GOOD pair that DIFFERS      the description or the tolerance
    (first line broken)           discriminates too finely. FALSE RED --
                                  loud, annoying, and self-reporting.

    a BAD one that MATCHES        the description or the tolerance is
    (second line broken)          too coarse. FALSE GREEN -- silent, and
                                  it cannot report itself.

D AND T ARE THE ONLY KNOBS, and section 5 names them: UNDERSTANDING
shapes D, TOLERANCE shapes T. So "understand what you can, tolerate only
what you cannot" is not taste -- it is the instruction for satisfying two
inequalities at once, using the transform that composes before reaching
for the relation that does not.

ACCEPTANCE IS NOT A FREE CHOICE. It is tempting to say the nominal's
identity does not matter -- if every good description matches every other,
any b0 in GOOD would serve. That is true and it is the wrong lesson,
because it demands the strictest possible form of the criterion and gives
nothing back.

TO ACCEPT IS TO DECLARE THE POLE OF GOODNESS: not any member of GOOD, but
the one all the others are arranged around. And the word already said so
-- a NOMINAL value, in engineering, is the designated centre with the
tolerance band around it. It was never 'whatever run we happened to
freeze'.

The gain is measurable. Around an ARBITRARY member, the band must span
the whole spread; around the POLE it need only reach the furthest member:

    arbitrary b0     T  >=  diameter( D(GOOD) )
    the POLE         T  >=  radius( D(GOOD) )        and radius <= diameter
                                                     <= 2 x radius

So the pole roughly HALVES the tolerance the criterion needs -- and every
unit of tolerance not spent is margin left over against BAD. The margin
condition becomes

    radius( D(GOOD) )  <=  T  <  distance( pole, D(BAD) )

which is a strictly easier thing to satisfy than the same statement about
an arbitrary representative.

    .......... D(GOOD) ..........
    o     o    o   X   o    o   o          X = the pole
          |<---- radius --->|              a band reaching the furthest
    |<-------- diameter -------->|         member, not spanning the spread

WHERE THE POLE IS NOT KNOWN, and usually it is not, acceptance is a
HYPOTHESIS: that this run stands at the centre. The hypothesis is
falsifiable, and a disagreement between two good runs is exactly what
falsifies it. So a test that starts failing on a second machine has not
found a bug in the code -- it has found that the accepted description was
not the pole, and the answer is to move the nominal toward the centre or
to widen D until the spread vanishes.

ACCEPTING A RUN NEAR THE EDGE IS A MISTAKE EVEN THOUGH THE RUN IS GOOD.
That is the practical content of all of this, and it is invisible without
it.

THE VERDICT MUST NOT DEPEND ON WHICH GOOD BEHAVIOUR WAS ACCEPTED. Written
out, that is a constraint on Eq itself:

    Eq(D(b0), D(b), T)  is not a function of  b0

The criterion already implies it -- if every good description matches
every other and none matches a bad one, then any b0 in GOOD decides
identically. So the danger is not that the rule is missing; it is that
NOTHING CHECKS IT, and a tolerance can break it quietly.

THE NUMERIC CASE, which is where it bites first. Accept 0.815 under a
RELATIVE tolerance and the band is centred on 0.815; accept 0.816 and it
is centred there instead. A value near the edge is then admissible or not
DEPENDING ON WHICH RUN HAPPENED TO BE ACCEPTED:

    accepted 0.815 |-----------o-----------|
    accepted 0.816     |-----------o-----------|
                                           ^
                                     inside one band,
                                     outside the other

Two demands follow.

  Eq MUST BE SYMMETRIC. 'within r of the NOMINAL' is not: it makes the
  nominal an anchor, and the criterion is not even well posed. A relative
  tolerance must be relative to something symmetric -- the larger
  magnitude, or the mean -- or be absolute.

  THE MARGIN CONDITION. Let d be the distance Eq thresholds, so that
  Eq(x, y, T) holds exactly when d(x, y) <= T. Since every comparison is
  against the POLE and never between two arbitrary members, the two lines
  of the criterion read

      radius( D(GOOD) )  <=  T  <  distance( pole, D(BAD) )

  and such a T EXISTS if and only if

      radius( D(GOOD) )  <  distance( pole, D(BAD) )

  -- the good descriptions must lie closer to their centre than that
  centre lies to any bad one. Where that holds, non-transitivity never
  bites, because nothing is ever chained: every comparison has the same
  fixed anchor.

AND THIS IS WHY UNDERSTANDING COMES FIRST. A canonicaliser that collapses
the varying part reduces the diameter of D(GOOD) TO ZERO, and then any
T >= 0 satisfies the left inequality and the choice of representative
cannot matter at all. Tolerance is needed exactly where D cannot do that
-- and there the margin must be checked, not assumed.

    radius 0          any T works; where the pole IS matters not at all
    radius > 0        T must fit between the spread about the pole and
                      the gap to BAD -- and somebody must know both

THE HEART OF THE OPERATION, then, is one expression:

    Equivalence( D(b_pole),  D(b),  T )

and its three parts are owned by three different parties. That division
is the whole architecture:

    Equivalence   its STRUCTURE      THE FRAMEWORK. The same for every
                  --------------     test ever written; no test may
                                     change how equivalence WORKS, only
                                     what it is given.

    D             the DESCRIPTION    THE TEST APPLICATION, in two pieces:
                  --------------     what it PRINTS, and the
                                     canonicaliser that rewrites that
                                     into canonical form. Both are the
                                     author's, and together they are D.

    T             the TOLERANCE      THE TESTER, as declared parameters.

    b_pole        the REFERENCE      THE STORE. Written by acceptance,
                  --------------     read by every comparison after.

THE FRAMEWORK OWNS NO JUDGEMENT. It supplies the machinery for both knobs
and chooses neither -- which is why every absorption is a CLAIM the
author makes and can be held to (section 8), rather than a behaviour the
tool exhibits.

AND THE TWO KNOBS ARE THE AUTHOR'S TOGETHER. D and T are not owned by
different people or held in different places: they are one decision about
one scenario, taken twice. Section 6 says which to reach for first;
nothing says they may be separated, and a design that files them apart
makes "what does this test absorb?" a question with two answers in two
places.

THE DISJOINTNESS IS A DEMAND ON THE AUTHOR. GOOD and BAD must not overlap
-- and where a specification leaves a behaviour both admissible and not,
no test of it can exist. The fault is then in the specification, and no
choice of D or T repairs it.


2  THE CHANNEL
______________________________________________________________________________

Behaviour is never inspected. It is expressed, measured, perceived, and only
the perception arrives. The path from the one to the other adds distortion:
the same behaviour, run twice, is perceived differently.

The channel cannot be removed. It can only be compensated for. Every
mechanism in HWUT exists for that single purpose.


3  THE TWO ARMS
______________________________________________________________________________

Judgement compares two perceptions, not a perception against truth:

    admissible behaviour --> channel --> perception --> freeing --> NOMINAL
                                                          ^
                                                     THE SAME MAP
                                                          |
    current behaviour ----> channel --> perception --> freeing --> SUBJECT

The nominal -- the GOOD file -- is one more perception, distorted by the
same channel, then frozen. It is evidence, not authority.

THE LAW OF THE ARMS: the freeing map is identical on both arms. Applied to
the subject alone, it compares an interpretation against a raw perception,
and the comparison means nothing.

Its corollary, where the nominal is stored already freed:

    free(NOMINAL) == NOMINAL          -- IDEMPOTENCE

A freeing that is not idempotent makes the arms asymmetric silently.


4  FREEING IS A QUOTIENT
______________________________________________________________________________

Compensating for the channel means declaring which differences are channel
and which are behaviour. Perceptions are thereby collected into classes, and
the classes -- not the perceptions -- are compared.

There are exactly two ways to build such a class.


5  THE TWO MEANS
______________________________________________________________________________

    UNDERSTANDING       sees the invariant behind the varying appearance.
                        Picks one CANONICAL REPRESENTATIVE of the class,
                        then compares BYTE-EXACT.
                        Equality after a function: reflexive, symmetric,
                        TRANSITIVE.
                        Carried by pype.

    TOLERANCE           compares pairwise within a band; no representative.
                        Reflexive, symmetric, NOT transitive --
                            a ~ b,  b ~ c,  yet  a !~ c
                        (two values each within epsilon of a third, two
                        epsilons apart). This is the mathematical tolerance
                        relation: congruence with transitivity removed.
                        Carried by compare.

Understanding loosens nothing. It changes the object, then judges it
exactly. Seeing through the disorder is what buys the strictness.
Tolerance loosens the criterion itself.


6  THE HIERARCHY
______________________________________________________________________________

    Understand what can be understood.
    Tolerate only what cannot.

A transitive quotient composes: classes may be built, nested and reasoned
about. A tolerance relation does not compose, so nothing can be built on top
of it. Choosing tolerance where understanding was available is therefore a
strict loss, not a matter of taste.

In the terms of 1.1: both knobs move the same two inequalities, but D
moves them by CHANGING THE OBJECT and T by WEAKENING THE RELATION. Only
the first leaves a relation that composes -- so reach for D first, and
for T only over what D cannot reach.

A suite heavy in tolerances and light in pype scripts is a suite whose
author declined to understand the output and bought a looser verdict
instead.


7  THE ASYMMETRY OF ERROR
______________________________________________________________________________

    admissible, but RED     the FIRST line of 1.1 broken. A nuisance.
                            Visible. Someone investigates.

    inadmissible, but GREEN the SECOND line broken -- the real failure,
                            invisible by construction: a passing test
                            reports nothing, and the evidence was
                            discarded before the comparison.

Every tolerance and every canonicalisation is therefore a CLAIM: what is
absorbed here is channel distortion, not behaviour. A wrong claim swallows a
real difference and the light stays green.

Hence: absorb the least that works.


8  DECLARATION
______________________________________________________________________________

A claim that is written down can be reviewed, argued with, and withdrawn. A
pype script and a stated tolerance are on the record. A matcher that absorbs
variation by inference makes the same claims invisibly, and no reader can
attack what no reader can see.

This, not superior leniency, is the argument for HWUT's shape.


9  DIRECTNESS
______________________________________________________________________________

The freeing happens in the mind of the tester. HWUT supplies the tools for
that mind, and is measured by the distance between what the tester knows and
what the tester must write:

    knows    "these six lines are a set, not a sequence"
    writes    potpourri                              -- distance ~ 0

    knows    "this timestamp is not behaviour"
    writes    a regex over the line shape            -- distance > 0:
                                                        mechanism, not meaning

THE CRITERION: the artefact is isomorphic to the thought.

Wherever the tester must speak in terms of the mechanism rather than the
meaning, the tool has failed -- however capable that mechanism is. This is
the review question to put to every construct in the language.


10  THE NAME
______________________________________________________________________________

Understanding and Tolerance are the differentiator. Every golden-master
framework has some tolerance mechanism; almost none declares an
understanding stage in front of the comparison, and so most buy leniency by
giving up exactness. HWUT buys exactness by understanding first.

"Unit test" names a category with a thousand members and hides all of the
above. U and T say what the thing is for; the letter order is also the
data flow and the epistemic order.
