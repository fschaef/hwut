The tolerance band belongs to the NOMINAL, the 'pole':

             band radius = abs(nominal) * ratio

             <---------|--------->
        -----+---------O---------+------------> number line
                    nominal            subject inside: EQUIVALENT

A radius carries no sign. The price of substituting subject for
nominal is 'max(|s-n| - band, 0) / max(|s|, |n|)', capped at 1.

ratio: 0.01
