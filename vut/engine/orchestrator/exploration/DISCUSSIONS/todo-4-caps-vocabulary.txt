todo-4  THE VOCABULARY OF 'caps'
______________________________________________________________________________

DECIDED (R-22): 'caps' is procsitter's scope, and procsitter owns every
default in it. DECIDED (R-47): the vocabulary is FIXED -- an unknown cap is
a fault at the door. DECIDED (R-48): a cap procsitter cannot enforce refuses
the test.

BUILT:  timeout_sec, network.

NAMED, UNBUILT -- each is one field in 'Caps', one branch in
'validator._caps', and one entry in 'RELATION':

    cpu_sec               CPU time, as distinct from wall clock
    memory_mb             address space
    file_size_mb          the largest file the process may write
    child_process_max_n   how many children it may spawn
    file_handle_max_n     how many handles it may hold open
    write_directory_list  where it may write. DEFAULT: the directory of
                          the test source and all of its
                          sub-directories.

Most map onto 'setrlimit' and are cheap. 'network' and
'write_directory_list' do not -- they need namespaces or a sandbox, and
procsitter may be unable to enforce them on a given platform.

WHERE A CAP CANNOT BE ENFORCED (R-48): the test does not run. The
documentation must state the way out:

    --ignore=<cap>    run the test with that cap unenforced, knowingly

The switch belongs to the RUNNER, not to this component. It is written down
here so that it is not lost: a refusal with no stated remedy is a wall.

OWED TO PROCSITTER (todo-1): the defaults of every cap above, declared on
its own face. The values in 'owner_faces_provisional.ConfigCaps' are
stand-ins and were not ruled one by one.
