[ ] (4) MAJOR: Complement operation, Fixation precedes Implication

    - Complement requires something stable, fixated, to operate on.
    - Becoming is in flux; nothing under top can be complemented.
    - Therefore Fixation must precede Implication.

      available (no fixated entity yet exists for complement to operate on).
    - After the first Fixation, complement becomes operational.
    - This is the seed of complementation, and eventually of time as
      perceived in discrete steps.
