3. The Reactor

The concrete and the abstract are driven by *Reactors*. A Reactor is the 
fundamental unit of change in its world. It holds an internal state called 
*Modality*.

Reactors transduce the immutable events of history into "The Now" with initial
events. They process this record against their Modality. This interaction
causes new events to exist in the subsequent Jiffy.

Without the Reactor, "The Now" remains empty. The Reactor transforms static 
History into dynamic Existence. The question of reactors being deterministic
or not is irrelevant to the structure of time and "The Now".

In the domain of the Reactor (the Concrete), time advances physically 
($dt > 0$).
