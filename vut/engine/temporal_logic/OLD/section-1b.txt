POSITIVE LOGIC AND IMPLICATION

Implications of events in the Obsnow are instantaneous and obey the 
*positive logic* of existence. That is, there is no operation of 'not 
event'. The 'not' operation is restricted to properties of the immutable 
history. In implications, properties of history can only appear in 'and' 
operations with events. In such a system *contradictions are impossible*. 

Let '<=' represent implication using the Positive Entailment. The
expression A <= B is read: 'The existence of A is implied by the existence
of B.' This operator is strictly monotonic: the existence of $B$ forces $A$
into existence, but the absence of $B$ exerts no force on $A$.
Given two properties of history 'ever()' and 'never()' with their intuitive 
meaning, consider the implications:

    (1) A <= B and ever(C)
    (2) A <= B and never(C)

When C ever existed, (1) causes the existence of A, but (2) remains silent. If
C never existed, then (2) causes the existence of A and (1) remains silent. In
"The Now," contradictions are impossible because the absence of an event is not
a conflicting state, such as 'False' vs. 'True'. Logical operations on history,
however, may consist of 'and', 'or', and 'not'.

STABILITY AND THE LEAST FIXED POINT

This system prevents logical circularities through Idempotence. An implication
can only be made based on the existence of its basis of events. Once an event
is brought into existence within "The Now," no subsequent implication can
remove it ("un-exist" it).

Because the logic of the Obsnow forbids the negation of events, the set of
existing events can only expand, never contract. This guarantees that the chain
of implications inevitably reaches a Least Fixed Point: a stable state where
all valid implications have been satisfied and the logical state comes to rest
within the single Jiffy. 

Consequently, circular dependencies (e.g., 'A <= B' and 'B <= A') do not cause
an infinite loop or instability. Instead, they resolve into the instantaneous,
stable existence of both A and B.

Once stability is reached (actually in a Jiffy of no-time), time progresses
and the set of events enters the record of immutable history.
