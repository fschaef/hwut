4. The Imp (The Implicator)

Counterposed to the Reactor is the *Imp*. While the Reactor operates 
*across* time, the Imp operates *inside* "The Now".

The Imp is blind to Modality. It observes only the surface of 'The Now'
(Events) and the immutable depth of History. It cannot perceive the internal
state of Reactors, ensuring that intra-Jiffy logic remains purely deductive and
never triggers state mutations.

The distinction is absolute:
* The Reactor causes *Events* (Time N -> Time N+1).
* The Imp provides *Meaning* (Time N -> Time N).

Together, they construct reality. The Reactor drives the sequence of what 
happens. The Imp expands the depth of what it means.

The Imp resolves the Transitive Closure of existence. This is not a temporal
process of 'becoming,' but a static realization of what 'is.' If A implies B,
and B implies C, then C exists simultaneously with A in the geometry of
"The Now".

In the domain of the Imp and The Now (the Abstract), physical time is frozen
($dt = 0$) while logical implication advances ($d\tau = 1$)
