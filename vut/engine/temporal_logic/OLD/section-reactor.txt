### 8. REACTORS

A **Reactor** is the engine’s organ of agency, serving as the bridge between
the immutable past and the potential future. While implications within the
Obsnow are "horizontal" (Event $\to$ Event), a Reactor is "vertical"—it
reflects upon the crystallized History to seed the next Jiffy.

#### The Edge of Reaction

A reaction is an event produced specifically upon a **change in state**. It is
the delta of interpretation that triggers agency. 

* **Example:** Two spheres move through space. Events enter the Obsnow in terms of
  `POSITION_OF_A` and `POSITION_OF_B`. By attributes of theses events (coordinates
  'x', 'y' and 'z') the observer may imply a new event, namely `COLLSITION_A_AND_B`.
  The underlying state change is 'non-touching' to 'touching'.

Notably, this triggerring can only occur a Jiffy after the Obsnow has received
the events about positions and committed them to history. 

#### Causal Agnosticism

The ontology remains strictly agnostic regarding the *basis* of a reaction. The
Reactor is a black box of agency. Whether the reaction is deterministic,
stochastic, teleological (goal-oriented), or an external actuator talented with
free will, the ontology does not care *why* the Reactor acts. It only asserts
*when* the action manifests: in the Jiffy immediately following the state
change.

> **[FORMALISM]**
> Let $\Psi(H_k)$ be the State Interpretation function.
> A Reactor $\rho$ fires an event $e$ at $k+1$ if and only if:
> $$\Psi(H_k) = \text{TRUE} \land \Psi(H_{k-1}) = \text{FALSE}$$
> The resulting event is added to the set of Caused Events $C(H_k)$, such that $e \in N_{k+1}$.

### 9. THREE PHASES OF THE CYCLE OF CAUSATION

The engine operates in a rhythmic "breath," a three-phase cycle that ensures
logical simultaneity is preserved within a Jiffy while physical succession is
enforced across them.

#### Phase I: Deduction (Simultaneity)

Caused events from the previous Jiffy pop into existence in the Obsnow. The
the internal belief system (the set of implications) potentially add events. Using
Positive Logic, the set of events expands monotonically until all valid
implications are satisfied. 

* **The Goal:** Reach the **Least Fixed Point** where the set of events is stable.

#### Phase II: Crystallization (Persistence)

The stable set of events ($N_k$) is committed to the immutable History ($H_k$).
This phase is the transition from the "flux" of the Now to the "granite" of the
Past. The history is now ready for an updated interpretation in terms of 
states. The ephemeral events are "integralized" into persistent States.

#### Phase III: Reaction (Succession)

Reactors evaluate the History in terms of States. They detect the
transitions—the rising and falling edges of states. Based on their internal
(and potentially non-deterministic) logic, they cause a new set of 
events. They pop into existence of the Obsnow in the next Jiffy. 

* **The Result:** The cycle closes and re-enters at Jiffy $k+1$.
