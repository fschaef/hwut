2. Reality: Concrete and Abstract

Reality manifests in two distinct aspects: the *concrete* and the *abstract*.
The *concrete* is the aspect of physical existence. The *abstract* is the
aspect of thought and structure. Changes of *modalities* in the abstract
and the concrete are the *causation* of existence of events in "The Now".

"The Now" acts as the *membrane* between these two aspects. The concrete and
the abstract never interact directly; they interact only by causing events
to exist in "The Now." and perceiving properties of history.

The interaction is governed by *Causation*, *Existence*, and *Perception*,
all happening in three successive Jiffies. 

1.  *Causation (Jiffy 1):* A modality in the concrete or the abstract
    changes, causing an event. This internal change happens in the darkness
    of the aspect's own state.

2.  *Existence (Jiffy 2):* The caused event exists in "The Now." Here, it
    is absolute and positive. It exists alongside other events and their
    immediate implications.

3.  *Perception (Jiffy 3):* The event has ceased to exist in "The Now" and
    is recorded in History. This causes a change in modality in the concrete
    or the abstract--a perceiption. 

This cycle operates on the scale of Jiffies. Since a Jiffy is infinitesimal
relative to physical time, the cycle of Causation $\to$ Existence $\to$
Perception occurs instantaneously from the perspective of the Concrete world.
The delay is purely topological, ensuring cause strictly precedes effect,
without introducing physical latency.

Neither the concrete nor the abstract perceives "The Now." They
perceive only History. Perception updates the inner state of the aspect,
which may lead to new causation, closing the loop. This temporal separation
allows the abstract to apply full-fledged logic (including negation and
temporal queries) to the perception of history, while "The Now" remains a
place of pure, contradiction-free existence.
