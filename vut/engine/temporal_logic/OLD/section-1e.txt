SCHROEDINGERS CAT?

The question of Schroedinger's cat has a trivial answer with the given
ontology. An observer is always bound to his Obsnow. He receives events which
are interpretations and implications that his mind produces. he sees absolute
reality is an illusion. The Obsnow, is the instance where undescribed reality
becomes information. Thus in the undescribed reality there is a truth to the
cat being alive or not. But the truth is not available unless an event appears
in the Obsnow. If no event arrives that informs about the implied state of the
cat, it does not mean that the cat is in a state of death and living at the
same time. It simply means that the truth behind it has not yet manifested
in an observer's Obsnow. Since the cat's property of observing it's own 
existince is tied to it's existence, in her Obsnow, the event exists.
