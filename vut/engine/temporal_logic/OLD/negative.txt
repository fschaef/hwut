---- Input from the Reviewer -------

You invoke the Knaster-Tarski Theorem to ground the **Unity of Apperception**.
For $N_k = \text{lfp}(\Phi)$ to be valid, your implication function $I$ must be
strictly monotonic. 

In your Section 3, you state: "It can add to the concept of the Now, but it
cannot subtract from it." While this satisfies the requirement for a
constructive logic, you must prove that your Categories (the rules in $I$) do
not contain hidden negations. In a Cyber-Physical System (CPS), "Sensor A =
Off" is often treated as the negation of "Sensor A = On." If your logic engine
treats these as mutually exclusive, then the introduction of "Sensor A = On"
would necessitate the removal of "Sensor A = Off," which violates monotonicity.

**The Critique:** If your system encounters a contradiction in the manifold,
and $I$ is truly monotonic, the LFP will simply contain both $P$ and $\neg P$
(an inconsistent set), rather than failing to converge. You call this
"Absurdity" in Section 5, but you must formally demonstrate that $\Phi$ remains
monotonic even when the manifold is "absurd."
