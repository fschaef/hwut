
ABSURDITY

The co-existence of implied events in the Obsnow does not express consistence of
information that they carry. In the frame of positive logic, they never
contradict, or un-exist each other. But the information carried by events, just
recorded in history, may still be absurd.

Absurdity may be caused by *unwanted behavior* of reality or by
*misimplication*. An *unwanted behavior* means that *reality* sends signals
that go against our expectations. For example, two sensors at a door, A and B
reporting about the state of the door. 

   DOOR_JUST_CLOSED <= SENSOR_A
   DOOR_JUST_OPENED <= SENSOR_B

An interpretation of history would be 'door is closed' and 'door is open' which
is impossible/absurd in common undestanding. Misimplication is caused by a
possible failure in the 'belief system' that underlies the implications. For
example given the set of implications of events:

    START_VERY_HAPPY          <= JUST_MARRIED
    START_TROUBLE             <= START_LIVING_WITH_A_WOMAN
    START_LIVING_WITH_A_WOMAN <= JUST_MARRIED
    START_VERY_UNHAPPY        <= START_TROUBLE

Imagine the event 'JUST_MARRIED' occurs, implications are made, and the Obsnow
leaves its traces in history. an interpretation of history would be 'I am
married', 'I am very happy', but also 'I am living with a woman', 'I am in
trouble', and 'I am very unhappy'. The states 'I am very happy' and 'I am very
unhappy' are absurd.

Note, that the notion of *absurdity* is also relative to another aspect of a
belief system, namely the *rules of consistency*. Only in a system where *being
happy* and *being unhappy* is inconsistent, the co-existence is absurd.

The 'belief system' expressed through the set of *implications of events*
and *consistency assertions* on states of history can be verified without
exposing the it to external events. But, only a consistent belief system
allows for a solid detection of *mis-interpretation* or *unwanted behavior* 
of the external.
