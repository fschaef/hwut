# 8.9 THE WORLD (The External Reality)

Definition:

	The WORLD is the totality of the external environment being monitored or
    stimulated. It is the source of all causation and the destination for
    all "to be injected" events. 

### 1. The Separation of Concerns

The WORLD exists independently of the Temporal Logic Engine. It operates on its
own timeline and follows its own physical or programmed laws. The Engine
remains ignorant of the internal state of the WORLD. It knows the state of the
WORLD solely through the events it emits.

The RULE BASE, the TWIN WORLD and the WORLD can only interact through events
that occur in "The Now". All observe "The Now" and receive their events from 
it. "The Now" becomes the syncronization point of events. It is where the 
junction of related entities happen.

Logically, events of the world must be injected by some interface into "The Now".
Vice versa, in order to inject events into the WORLD, some type of listener
must perceive "The Now" and accordingly, sent the information to the WORLD.

At the moment of perception, the event ceases to exist in "The Now." It
survives only as information within the perceiver (WORLD interface, the TWIN
WORLD agent, or the RULE BASE instance). This ensures that the Engine remains
reactive and never processes "stale" reality.

NOTE ---------------------------------------------------------------
| By making "The Now" the singular synchronization point (the "common ground"),
| you’ve solved the problem of how three different domains (Logic, Simulation,
| and Reality) can interact without violating each other's internal laws.
