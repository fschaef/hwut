THE LOCO (Logical Consequence)

Definition:

    A LOCO is a set of persistent logical implications. It is a functional 
    property of "The Now" that determines how events, history, and the 
    TWIN WORLD interact to imply instanteneously other events.

To ensure absolute determinism, the LOCO follows a strict causal timeline that
separates the *cause* of a rule from the Implications of that rule:

- CAUSATION (J_n): An event occurs in "The Now" that satisfies a 
  LOCO's trigger condition.

- MANIFESTATION (J_n+1): The LOCO becomes an active property of 
  "The Now." It only begins to imply events from "The Now" to
  produce other events.

- CEASATION (J_m): An event occurs in "The Now" that satisfies 
  the LOCO's termination condition.

- PERISHMENT (J_m+1): The LOCO no longer implies events.

This buffer ensures a LOCO never reacts to its own birth-event, 
preserving a clean causal chain.

DETERMINISM through possitive logic
===========

The Engine utilizes "Positive Event Logic," meaning implications are
triggered with repsect to "The Now" solely by the existence of events. 

- Because there is no "NOT Event" operator within a Jiffy, multiple 
  LOCOs manifesting in the same "Now" cannot contradict one another.

- Logic is additive: every LOCO in "The Now" contributes its 
  implications to the totality of the engine's perception.

The violation (Primary objective)

A Violation is an Implied Event. It is the fundamental task of the temporal
logic engine to detect those violations.


Technical Aspects
------------------
* THE ACTOR: A LOCO is anchored to a specific identity (name/alias) 
    defined at the moment of Causation.

* INDEPENDENCE: Thousands of LOCOs may exist in "The Now" simultaneously; 
    their positive-logic nature ensures they function as independent 
    parallel processors of reality.
