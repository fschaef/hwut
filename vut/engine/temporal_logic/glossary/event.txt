NOW: 
====

Definition:

   - "The Now" is the current, ephemeral, infinitesimal point in time where
     information is real.

   - Its temporal window is exactly one Jiffy (ε) of time.

   - It only manifests through **Events** that exist within it. If no event 
     exists, "The Now" is a void.

   - Events of "The Now" perish as soon as "The Now" enters the next Jiffy.

   - An implied event exists in the same "Now" as the event that implies it.

In particular, 'History' is not an event and is not perceived in itself as a
reality of "The Now". An event, however, might occur when history is brought
in relation with an event. 

If an event $E_1$ implies a Meta-Event $E_2$, then $E_2$ is born into the exact
same "Now" as $E_1$. Logical implications do not loose the context of the
causal event, but share the same Jiffy.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MATHEMATICAL DEFINITION (The Momentary Perspective)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Let Θ be "The Now" at (t, ε).
Let E_now be the set of events e such that e ∈ Θ.

1. THE PERISH FUNCTION:
   ∀e ∈ Θ(t, ε) : e ∉ Θ(t, ε + 1)
   (Events exist in one and only one Jiffy).

2. THE IMPLICATION RULE:
   If E_1 ∈ Θ and E_1 ⟹ E_2, then E_2 ∈ Θ.
   (Causal implications are simultaneous within the Jiffy).

3. THE PERCEPTION OF HISTORY (H):
   H is a function of the Persistent State S_w.
   A "Fact" (F) is only perceivable in Θ if:
   F = f(e, S_w) where e ∈ E_now.
   (History is invisible until an event 'invokes' it from the TwinWorld).

EVENT
=====

Definition:

    An Event is a discrete, immutable, packet of information. It 
    exists only in "The Now" and its lifespan is that of a Jiffy. 

    An Event exist by either 'Causation' from the previous "Now" 
    or 'Implication' of other Events.

An event is the fundamental unit of communication and or perceiption between
entities. An event is potentially pertained in 'history'. But, as
defined earlier, history only becomes part of "The Now" when implied in
conjuction with an event.

The *causation* of an event, the *existence* of an event itself, and the
*perceiption* happen in three distinct, subsequent Jiffies. The *perceiption*
may eventually become a *causation* for another event. The direct logical
*implication* happens instantaneously in the save Jiffy of the events that
imply it.

NOTE ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
| By enforcing that Perception happens in the Jiffy after Existence, we prevent
| "Infinite Feedback Loops" within a single Jiffy. It creates a stable,
| clock-like progression where the engine can never get stuck in a "now" that
| won't end; it must always move to the next Jiffy to perceive what it just did.

Technical Aspects
------------------
The Event Object:

   * NAME:       Name of the Event, or its category.

   * ATTRIBUTES: A flexible data structure (accessible in Lua) 
                 containing the specific values (e.g., ID, price, velocity),
                 this may also include any type of meta-data.

   * TIME:       The temporal anchor, timestamp (T) representing its position 
                 on the Simulated Physical Timeline.

Events are caused from the WORLD and enter the Now of the engine. As such they
are visible to the RULE SPACE and the TWIN WORLD. The TWIN WORLD may inject 
events into "The Now" which are injected into the WORLD.

HISTORY
=======

Definition:

	History is the immutable memory of all events that occurred before the
    current "Now." Association of existing event in "The Now" with of 
    properties of history with generate instanteous events living in 
    the same Jiffy of "Now".

It is the structural foundation that allows the engine to reason about, or to
perceive, time. While the "Now" is *ephemeral*, History is *permanent*.

Technical Aspects
-----------------

Any event, from the WORLD, implied or caused by the TWIN WORLD, has an Id.
History maintains are record of the occurence of any event together with
its attributes.
