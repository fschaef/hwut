# 8. Formal Glossary & The Jiffy Calculus

This section codifies the temporal mechanics of the Engine, utilizing a non-standard model of time to resolve the tension between physical simultaneity and logical causality.

### 8.1 The Jiffy Calculus (Infinitesimal Dynamics)
The Jiffy (ε) is defined as a non-zero, infinitesimal unit of logical time. It acts as the "differential" of the testing environment, allowing for state evolution without physical time progression.

* **The Non-Archimedean Axiom:** For any two physical time steps t1, t2 where t1 < t2, the distance Δt = t2 - t1 is a Standard Real number. The Jiffy ε is an infinitesimal such that:
    Σ (i=1 to ∞) ε < Δt
    No finite or countably infinite amount of logical "chaining" can bypass the physical progression of the system.
* **The Principle of Evolution (t + ε):** * In the Physical Domain: t1 and (t1 + ε) are indistinguishable. The reported timestamp is identical.
    * In the Logical Domain: t1 and (t1 + ε) are strictly ordered (t1 ≺ t1 + ε).
    * Like the 'dx' in dynamic systems, ε is crucial for the evolvement of the system state; the state at (t + ε) is a direct consequence of the state and actions at (t).

### 8.2 Definitions

**Guard**
The primary unit of behavioral validation. A Guard is a temporal container instantiated to monitor a specific lifecycle. It consists of:
* **TSCOPE:** The life-span definition (on/until triggers).
* **@new:** The Spawn handler (initialization and stimulus injection).
* **@delete:** The Destruction handler (the "passing away" and final settlement).
* **Rules:** The set of temporal invariants active only during the Guard's life.

**Jiffy**
An infinitesimal unit of logical time. It represents a single "micro-step" of causality. The engine evaluates the world in Jiffies to ensure that "Reactions" are logically distinct from "Triggers," even when they share a physical timestamp.

**Causal Chain**
A sequence of Jiffies (J1, J2, ... Jn) initiated by an external event. The chain continues until the Rule Space reaches a **Fixpoint** (a state where no further internal events or state changes are generated). The engine must reach this fixpoint before accepting the next external event.

**Shadow Model**
A reference representation of the System Under Test (SUT) maintained in Lua. Like a dynamic system state, it evolves step-by-step through Jiffies. It is updated during @new, @delete, or via state-change triggers.

**The Zero-Width Property**
The guarantee that all logical reasoning, state updates, and command injections occurring within a Causal Chain occupy zero width on the **Simulated Physical Timeline** (the timeline reported by the SUT's timestamps). This ensures the engine remains a transparent observer that does not skew the SUT's own reported timing.

**Parameterization (Anchoring)**
The mechanism of binding a Guard instance to a specific data value (e.g., RequestID). This provides **Isolation**: the engine automatically routes events to the correct Guard instance, ensuring that concurrent lifecycles do not interfere with one another.

**Fixpoint**
The stable state of the Rule Space where all logical dependencies, Meta-Events, and internal signals have been resolved for the current Jiffy, and no further internal events are pending.
