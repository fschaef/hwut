
In the Synthetic Interstice, ephemeral {\it Events\/} pop into existence. {\it
Original Events\/} are instantiated by the Noumena and {\it Implied Events\/} are
`necessitated' by the rules of nature~\footnote{Here `nature' shall designate the
structure that exists without the creational intervention of the Cognitive
Subject.}. Events are the smallest entity of information that allows for the
perception of {\it Identity}, i.e.\ the smallest unit that allows to
differentiate a $\delta_a$ from a $\delta_b$. Events solely exist in the gap in time, the
Synthetic Interstice---thus have no temporal expansion. 

The Synthetic Interstice, the gap in time, is where any dynamic perceived by
the Cognitive Subject originates from. It is the closest interface to absolute
reality. It is where reality manifests. When its atemporal operations reach a
fixed point, the fixation/writing of the resulting Events marks the end of the
Synthetic Interstice and the beginning of a new. The distinction between the
purely dynamic, non-writing, nature of the Synthetic Interstice and the
written, immutable, nature of History has a significant impact on the
operational logic of the Manifold.

The {\it fixation\/} of events into History marks the end of a Synthetic
Interstice. During the purely dynamic Synthetic Interstice, no `write operations'
happen. The operations during the Synthetic Interstice rely solely on the 
truth of '{\it Becoming\/}'. In contrast, History expresses the truth of `{\it
Has-Become\/}'. Fixation marks the boundary between the dynamic present of
the Synthetic Interstice and the immutable History. This has significant impact
on the logic that may be applied during the Synthetic Interstice.
