===============================================================================
disc-3  CAUSE REFERENCE CARRYING AN EXTRA GUARD  ('<ref>(args) & <guard>')
===============================================================================
SCOPE     semantic (pass-2). Grammar admits it; meaning undecided.

THE SHAPE:
    '<name-dotted>(args) & <guard>' PARSES -- one merged grammar shape decides
    reference-vs-trigger by the parens. So a cause REFERENCE may syntactically
    carry an extra '& <guard>'.

OPEN -- what it means:
    No semantics assigned. Until decided, pass 2 reports a cause reference
    carrying '&' as FATAL [STRUCTURE] (admitted by the grammar, refused by the
    checker).

CANDIDATE MEANING:
    Conjunction with the referenced definition's own guard (the extra guard
    AND-ed onto the definition's condition). Not adopted -- needs a decision on
    whether a reference may tighten a definition's guard, and how the combined
    guard reads against the definition's event binding.

ENTRY CONDITION:
    Decide when a use actually wants to constrain a referenced cause at the
    reference site. Either assign the conjunction meaning or keep the FATAL
    refusal as the permanent answer.
