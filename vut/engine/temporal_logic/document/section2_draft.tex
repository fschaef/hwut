\section{Truth Modalities}

The first Information Substance $\delta_\bullet$---at the moment of its
instantiation---stands alone. There is no other discernible, no counterpart
with which it could interact. Without interaction, no further dynamic can
develop from $\delta_\bullet$ on its own.

Yet $\delta_\bullet$ does not vanish. The truth it carries does not cease to be
true; it merely shifts from \textit{Becoming} to \textit{Having-Become}, with
the discernible itself preserved intact. We distinguish these two modes as
truth modalities: $\top$ for \textit{Becoming}, $\bot$ for
\textit{Having-Become}. We name the states of a discernible \textit{Event} and
\textit{Atomic} Fact, and the transition between them Fixation.

\begin{formalism}[Event]
    An \textbf{Event} is a discernible coupled with the truth modality $\top$:
    \begin{equation}
        e := \langle \top, \delta \rangle
    \end{equation}
    It is the discernible in the mode of \textit{Becoming}. It is
    \textit{ephemeral} in nature.
\end{formalism}

We consider the pair $\langle \top, \delta \rangle$ as the closest possible
conceptual representation of the Event as an entity in the realm of
pre-conceptual Information Substance. It expresses Truth Modality and
Information Substance as if independent.

\begin{formalism}[Atomic Fact]
    An \textbf{Atomic Fact} is a discernible coupled with the truth modality
    $\bot$:
    \begin{equation}
        a := \langle \bot, \delta \rangle
    \end{equation}
    It is the discernible in the mode of \textit{Having-Become}---fixated,
    immutable, no longer subject to logical dynamic.
\end{formalism}

\begin{formalism}[Fixation]
    \textbf{Fixation} is the transition of an Event into an Atomic Fact:
    \begin{equation}
        \langle \top, \delta \rangle \rightarrow \langle \bot, \delta \rangle
    \end{equation}
    The truth modality shifts from $\top$ to $\bot$; the discernible is
    preserved.
\end{formalism}

Becoming ($\top$) is ephemeral in the strictest sense---the state between
not-being-yet and being, with nothing in between them to fill. It is the
sharp edge where non-existence passes into existence. Once the entity is,
Becoming is complete, and its state differs from the state during
instantiation: it is now $\bot$. The discernible $\delta_\bullet$, though,
still carries the truth it has carried from the primordial moment beyond any
time. Its truth does not change, only its modality. What has become true as
an Information Substance remains stable under the Absence of Redundancy: no
other substance can carry this truth, and so what has become true remains
true---immutable.

The truth carried by an Event originates from beyond time, in the
Transconceptual. Three connected properties follow from this trans-temporal
origin:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textit{Invariance of meaning.} The truth does not alter; what is
    true does not become untrue.
    \item \textit{Immutability.} Once a truth has become, it remains. No
    mechanism can undo Becoming.
    \item \textit{Positive existence.} There is no inverse operation; no
    Information Substance vanishes back into nothing.
\end{itemize}

These properties belong to the information substance, the discernible, which
carries the truth and provides identity. Event and Atomic Fact are the two
modalities in which a discernible exists; the discernible is the same in both,
and so are its properties.

Importantly, these properties categorically separate Information Substance from
Concepts. The transience of concepts---the live and death of things, the
appearance and disappearance of names and ideas---is not coherent with these
properties. Concepts require a later detailed investigation.
