\section{The Primordial Event}

The first Information Substance $\delta_\bullet$---at the moment of its
instantiation---stands alone. There is no other discernible, no counterpart
with which it could interact. Without interaction, no further dynamic can
develop from $\delta_\bullet$ on its own.

Yet $\delta_\bullet$ does not vanish. The truth it carries does not cease to
be true; it merely shifts from \textit{Becoming} to \textit{Having-Become},
with the discernible itself preserved intact. We distinguish these two modes
as truth modalities: $\top$ for \textit{Becoming}, $\bot$ for
\textit{Having-Become}.

\begin{formalism}[Event]
    An \textbf{Event} is a discernible coupled with the truth modality $\top$:
    \begin{equation}
        e := \langle \top, \delta \rangle
    \end{equation}
    It is the discernible in the mode of \textit{Becoming}. It is
    \textit{ephemeral} in nature.
\end{formalism}

\begin{formalism}[Atomic Fact]
    An \textbf{Atomic Fact} is a discernible coupled with the truth modality
    $\bot$:
    \begin{equation}
        a := \langle \bot, \delta \rangle
    \end{equation}
    It is the discernible in the mode of \textit{Having-Become}---fixated,
    immutable, no longer subject to logical dynamic.
\end{formalism}

\begin{formalism}[Fixation]
    \textbf{Fixation} is the transition of an Event into an Atomic Fact:
    \begin{equation}
        \langle \top, \delta \rangle \rightarrow \langle \bot, \delta \rangle
    \end{equation}
    The truth modality shifts from $\top$ to $\bot$; the discernible is
    preserved.
\end{formalism}

Fixation is structurally fundamental. At the new logical step reached through
Fixation, a further truth emerges: the fixated $\delta_\bullet$ does not
comprise the Transconceptual. By the tight link between truth and Information
Substance, this truth exists as a new Event carrying this fact at the new
logical step.



It is the mechanism that partitions the
logical dynamic into discrete steps. Two elements of logical dynamic must
therefore be distinguished: \textit{implications}, which act within a single
step among Events under $\top$; and \textit{fixations}, which close a step by
transitioning Events into Atomic Facts and thereby open the way for the next.
Without fixation, there would be no boundary between steps---no separation
between what is currently the case and what has been the case. The discreteness
of logical steps, and ultimately the discreteness of time, follows from this
mechanism.


The first Event $e_\bullet := \langle \top, \delta_\bullet \rangle$ thus
fixates into the first Atomic Fact $a_\bullet := \langle \bot, \delta_\bullet
\rangle$. The truth carried by $\delta_\bullet$---that the Transconceptual
exists, that the Receptivity exists, that the Transconceptual is active and
the Receptivity passive---remains true. It has merely passed from active
manifestation into immutable record.
