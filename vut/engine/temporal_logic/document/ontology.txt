# THE ONTOLOGY OF THE TEMPORAL LOGIC ENGINE

### 1. EVENTS AND THE NOW

A *Jiffy* is the abstraction of a discrete step—a logical cut in the temporal
continuum. It is a unit of simultaneity with zero physical duration, serving as
the interval wherein all logical implications reach a *fixed point*.

> **[FORMALISM]**
> Let $\mathbb{T} = \mathbb{N}_0$ be the set of discrete time steps (Jiffies).
> Let $k \in \mathbb{T}$ denote the current Jiffy index.

An *event* is an atomic unit of information existing in "The Observed Now"
(Obsnow). An event is either *caused* or *implied*. A caused event exists the
Obsnow of the Jiffy immediately after it's causation. An implied event manifests in
the Obsnow instantaneously via deduction. The event carries meaning solely
relative to an observer: an observer derives meaning through *interpretation*
(of caused events) and a *belief system* (the set of implications applied).

> **[FORMALISM]**
> Let $\mathbb{E}$ be the finite set of all possible event symbols (the Universe of Discourse).
> The **Obsnow** at step $k$ is a set $N_k \subseteq \mathbb{E}$.
> Let $C: \mathcal{H} \to \mathcal{P}(\mathbb{E})$ be the **Causation Function** mapping previous history to initial events in $k$.
> Let $I: \mathcal{P}(\mathbb{E}) \to \mathcal{P}(\mathbb{E})$ be the **Implication Function** (deduction within $k$).

The Obsnow is singular and persistent; it is the vessel that holds the
*existence* of events. Events, together with their implications, inhabit the
Obsnow for exactly one Jiffy—the logical instant required for the set to
saturate and stabilize. The set of stable events is then committed to
*history*. *History* is the immutable sequence of event sets that have existed
in the Obsnow:

> **[FORMALISM]**
> Let $\mathbb{E}$ be the Universe of Events and $k \in \mathbb{N}_0$ be the current Jiffy index.
> **History** $H_k$ is defined as the immutable, ordered tuple of event sets up to step $k$:
>
> $$H_k := \langle N_0, N_1, \dots, N_k \rangle \in (\mathcal{P}(\mathbb{E}))^{k+1}$$
>
> Where:
> * $N_i \subseteq \mathbb{E}$ is the set of events at Jiffy $i$.
> * The **Crystallization** (transition) from $k$ to $k+1$ is defined by appending the new stable set:
>     $$H_{k+1} = H_k \frown \langle N_{k+1} \rangle$$

All events and implications in the Obsnow are topologically co-located. Logical
implications are instantaneous, independent of the physical location of the
subject. All of reality collapses for the moment of a Jiffy into the Obsnow as
a realm of meaning.

The Obsnow hosts events for a Jiffy; new events exist in the *next* Jiffy only
through *causation*. Without causation, the Obsnow is empty of events—and thus
empty of meaning. The *causation* of an event and its existence in the Obsnow
occur in two successive Jiffies ($k$ and $k+1$).

### 2. POSITIVE LOGIC AND IMPLICATION

Implications within the Obsnow operate under *positive logic*. Existence is
strictly additive; there is no operation of 'not event' or 'anti-event'.
Negation is explicitly barred from the Obsnow. The 'not' operation is
restricted solely to queries against the immutable *history*.

> **[FORMALISM]**
> The Implication Function $I(S)$ is **Monotonic**:
> $$\forall A, B \subseteq \mathbb{E} : A \subseteq B \implies I(A) \subseteq I(B)$$
> Negation ($\neg e$) is undefined for $e \in N_k$.
> Negation is valid only for predicates $P(H)$ over history.

In implications, properties of history serve as predicates (gates), but the
result of an implication is always the *addition* of an event. In such a
system, *contradictions are structurally impossible*.

Let `$\Leftarrow$` represent Positive Entailment. The expression $A \Leftarrow B$ is
read: 'The existence of A is implied by the existence of B.' This operator is
strictly monotonic: the presence of $B$ forces $A$ into existence, but the
absence of $B$ exerts no force on $A$. It is silence, not negation.

> **[FORMALISM]**
> An implication rule $r$ is a pair $(Conditions, Consequent)$.
> $$Condition \subseteq \mathbb{E} \cup \{ P(H) \}$$
> $$Consequent \in \mathbb{E}$$
> The update step is strictly additive: $S_{next} = S_{current} \cup \{ e \mid (Cond, e) \in Rules \land Cond \subseteq S_{current} \}$

Given two properties of history, `ever()` and `never()`, consider the
implications:

    (1) A <= B and ever(C) 
    (2) A <= B and never(C)

If `C` exists in history, (1) causes the existence of `A`, while (2) remains
silent. If `C` never existed, then (2) causes the existence of `A` and (1)
remains silent.

In the Obnow, contradictions cannot arise because the absence of an event is
not a logical value (False) that conflicts with presence (True); it is simply
null. While logical operations on history may utilize 'and', 'or', and 'not' to
form predicates, the Obsnow remains a pure vessel of existence.

### 3. STABILITY AND THE LEAST FIXED POINT

This system eliminates logical instability through Idempotence and
Monotonicity. An implication is a constructive force; it can only add to the
set of existing events. Once an event is brought into existence within "The
Now," no subsequent implication can remove it.

> **[FORMALISM]**
> Let $\Phi(S) = S \cup I(S)$ be the step function for a Jiffy.
> Since $S \subseteq \Phi(S)$ (Inflationary) and $\Phi$ is Monotonic, by the **Knaster-Tarski Theorem**:
> $$N_k = \text{lfp}(\Phi) = \bigcup_{i=0}^{\infty} \Phi^i(C(H_{k-1}))$$
> Since $\mathbb{E}$ is finite, convergence is guaranteed in finite steps.

Because the logic of the Obsnow forbids the negation of current events, the set
of existing events is strictly monotonically increasing during the calculation
of a Jiffy. This guarantees that the chain of implications inevitably reaches a
Least Fixed Point: a stable state where all valid implications are satisfied
and the set ceases to change.

Consequently, circular dependencies (e.g., 'A <= B' and 'B <= A') do not
produce infinite loops or oscillation. Instead, they resolve into the
instantaneous, stable existence of the union of the participants (both A and
B).

Stability defines the Jiffy. The moment the set of events stops expanding
($S_{i+1} = S_i$), the Jiffy is complete, and the set enters the record of
immutable history.

### 4. ABSURDITY

The co-existence of implied events in the Obsnow guarantees logical stability,
but it does not guarantee semantic consistency. In the frame of positive logic,
events never contradict or "un-exist" each other. However, the information
carried by these events, once recorded in history, may describe a state that
the observer considers *absurd*.

Absurdity is not a system error; it is a divergence between *System State* and
*Observer Expectation*. It arises from two sources:

1.  **Unwanted Behavior (Reality Divergence):** This occurs when external
    reality emits signals that contradict physical constraints. For example,
    consider two door sensors, A and B:
    
    DOOR_JUST_CLOSED <= SENSOR_A 
    DOOR_JUST_OPENED <= SENSOR_B

    If both sensors fire in the same Jiffy, the engine faithfully records both
    events. The interpretation—'door is closed' AND 'door is open'—is
    physically impossible, yet logically valid within the engine. The engine
    has correctly captured a sensor failure.

2.  **Misimplication (Belief Divergence):** This occurs when the 'belief
    system' (the set of implications) contains flawed logic that permits
    inconsistent states. Consider this chain:

    START_VERY_HAPPY          <= JUST_MARRIED 
    START_TROUBLE             <= START_LIVING_WITH_A_WOMAN 
    START_LIVING_WITH_A_WOMAN <= JUST_MARRIED
    START_VERY_UNHAPPY        <= START_TROUBLE

    If 'JUST_MARRIED' occurs, the Obsnow saturates with both 'START_VERY_HAPPY'
    and 'START_VERY_UNHAPPY'. The engine does not crash; it simply presents the
    paradox.

> **[FORMALISM]**
> Let $\mathcal{K}$ be a set of Consistency Constraints (e.g., mutually exclusive sets).
> A Jiffy $N_k$ is **Valid** iff: $N_k = \text{lfp}(\Phi)$.
> A Jiffy $N_k$ is **Absurd** iff: $\exists \kappa \in \mathcal{K} : N_k \not\models \kappa$.
> The engine computes Validity; the user enforces Consistency.

Note that *absurdity* is relative to a specific set of *Consistency Rules*. A
state is only absurd if there exists a rule declaring the co-existence of those
specific elements to be invalid. The engine computes the *existence* of the
state; the belief system determines the *validity* of the state.

### 5. STATE AND HISTORY

History is the immutable, ordered sequence of all sets of events that have ever
existed in the Obsnow. It is the deep memory of the system. $$H = \langle N_0,
N_1, \dots, N_k \rangle$$

A *State* is not an event; it is a derived *Interpretation* of History. The
definition of a *State* intrinsically demands persistence—a non-zero duration
that survives the transition between Jiffies.

> **[FORMALISM]**
> Let $\mathbb{S}$ be the set of possible States.
> A State Interpretation is a function $\Psi: (\mathcal{P}(\mathbb{E}))^* \to \mathbb{S}$.
> State $S_k = \Psi(H_k)$.
> Unlike events, states persist: $S_k$ remains valid until $H_{k+n}$ changes the evaluation of $\Psi$.

To maintain the uniformity of the engine, State Interpretations are also
expressed as implications (Positive Logic), but their domain is the *History*,
not just the current Jiffy.

    STATE_PROPERTY <= PREDICATE(HISTORY)

For example, to interpret the state 'LIGHT_IS_ON' from a history of 'TURN_ON'
and 'TURN_OFF' events:

    LIGHT_IS_ON <= ever(TURN_ON) and ( never(TURN_OFF) or last(TURN_ON) >
    last(TURN_OFF) )

Any implication derived solely from the Obsnow (e.g., `LIGHT_IS_ON <= TURN_ON`)
precipitates a logical collapse: the inferred state vanishes the instant the
causing event clears (the subsequent Jiffy). The persistent nature of a *State*
is the symmetric inverse of the ephemeral nature of an *Event*. To achieve
persistence, the implication must be anchored in *History*, bridging the
discrete intervals between Jiffies until a counter-event in the record
invalidates the predicate.

Thus, the Obsnow is the generator of Truth (Events), and History is the
sustainer of Meaning (State).

### 6. SCHRÖDINGER'S CAT AND RELATIVISTIC TRUTH

The paradox of Schrödinger's cat finds its resolution in the strict constraints
of the Obsnow. The engine does not model an omniscient "God's Eye View"; it
models the *Local Truth* of a specific observer.

> **[FORMALISM]**
> Let $\mathcal{O}$ be the set of Observers.
> Truth is indexed by observer: $Truth(e, o, k) \iff e \in N_{k, o}$.
> Global Truth ($\exists k : e \in N_{k, \text{God}}$) is undefined in this ontology.

An observer has no source of truth other than the events manifested in their
Obsnow. In this sense, the Obsnow is absolute. However, the events themselves
are merely *interpretations* extracted from "The Raw."

"The Raw" is the substrate that connects all Obsnows. It is absolute, yet it
does not possess properties tangible to information-based reasoning. It
manifests *in* information and events, but it does not *consist* of them. The
Obsnow acts as a reducing valve, collapsing this blinding Absolute Truth into
manageable, discrete *Facts* (Events).

> **[FORMALISM]**
> Let $\mathcal{R}$ be The Raw (The Noumenon). $\mathcal{R}$ is not a set.
> The Obsnow is a projection function $\Pi_o: \mathcal{R} \to \mathcal{P}(\mathbb{E})$.
> Distinct observers imply distinct projections: $\Pi_{cat} \neq \Pi_{scientist}$.

Crucially, "The Raw" connects observers through Obsnows, but their *Truths*
remain distinct. The "Cat" is an observer with its own Obsnow.

1.  **In the Cat's Obsnow:** The state `I_AM_ALIVE` is derivable from its local
history. The truth is immediate and absolute. The Cat perceives no
superposition. 

2.  **In the Scientist's Obsnow:** The box is closed. No event `CAT_DEAD` or
`CAT_ALIVE` has been caused. The necessary manifestation of the truth of "The
Raw" has not yet put the scientist in a position of knowing.

The paradox of superposition (Life and Death co-existing) is a fallacy of
conflating the Map (the Obsnow) with the Territory (The Raw). The Scientist
cannot access the Cat's Obsnow or history. In the Scientist's Obsnow, the cat's
state is not "Both"—it is *Unknown*.

This imposes a critical constraint on logic: **Scientific Reality is not
Absolute Truth; it is merely a bound Obsnow.** To be rigorous, our logic must
accept the 'Unknown' as a valid state. We reject the binary assumption that
"Not True implies False." Falsehood requires evidence (an event) just as Truth
does. Without an event, a proposition is neither True nor False; it is
*Undeterminable*.

> **[FORMALISM]**
> **Ternary Logic:**
> Value $(p) = \begin{cases} \text{True} & \text{if } p \in N_k \\ \text{False} & \text{if } \text{evidence}(\neg p) \in N_k \\ \text{Unknown} & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$

Thus, objective reality is an unreachable, unstructured Absolute. We are left
only with the localized, discrete certainty of our own events.
