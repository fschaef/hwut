\section{The Noumena and the Cognitive Subject}

We begin with an absolute demarcation: the \textit{Physical Universe} is
strictly an instance of a \textit{Conceptual Universe}. Physics is a discipline
of models, and models are conceptual architectures. Therefore, an inquiry into
the origin of reality cannot commence with physical time, space, or matter
(e.g., the Big Bang), as these concepts inherently presuppose a cognitive
framework to be intelligible. Physics is, by necessity, a downstream construct.

Our initial axiom is the axiom of \textit{Finite Receptivity}~\cite{Kant1781}.
Concepts necessitate a substance to exist upon. Absolute Truth, the Noumena,
does not. So we posit that at the origin, only the Noumena exists. In the
absence of conceptuality, there are no conceptualizable answers to how or why
the un-conceptualizable Noumena exists, because conceptual reasoning is
restricted to the world of conceptions. The Noumena exists beyond. Furthermore,
there is no separation, no categorization, and no multitude within the Noumena,
as plurality requires differentiation, and differentiation requires concepts to
be compared. 

As Kant rigorously demonstrated, the thing-in-itself, the Noumena, remains
forever beyond the grasp of the Cognitive Subject. It is not a ``gap-filler''
for the yet-unexplained, but rather the absolute boundary condition of human
cognition. Yet, while it cannot be contained within concepts, the Noumena is
strictly not \textit{Nothingness}. 

Nothingness is the absolute absence of anything. This absolute absence would
include the absence of any capacity to act as a trigger. Nothingness cannot
trigger the basis of existence; it cannot trigger the genesis of logic, of
time, of physical laws, nor the manifestation of matter and energy. The
Noumena, conversely, is the un-modeled \textit{Ground Reality} from which the
trigger of existence inevitably springs.

This text proposes a mathematical framework for describing the metaphysical
process of cosmogony. We aim to identify the mathematical mechanics at the
heart of our perception of the universe---mechanics which evade physical
observation. The cosmogony is developed strictly through the necessities of
concepts and their consistency.

\section{The Primordial Event}

We must begin from a conceptual void, where only the Noumena exists: an
un-conceptualizable Absolute Truth---a ground reality---an existence strictly
independent of conceptualization. At this origin point, no dynamic, no
fixation, and nothing that could constitute time is assumed to exist; there is
only the initial fundamental atomic unit of conceptual nature: an {\it
Event\/}---caused by the only existing candidate of action, namely the 
Noumena. 

\begin{formalism}[Event and Discernible]

    An \textbf{Event} $e\in \mathbb{E}$ is the instantiation of a `concept',
    i.e.\ it is the instantiation of information content. It is something that
    can be experienced/perceived, that `informs'. It has no temporal expansion. 

    An Event consists of two linked properties, namely the truth of its own
    existence $\top$ and a {\it discernible\/} $\delta \in \mathbb{D}$, the
    `information content' by means of which it possesses identity.

    \begin{equation}
        e := \langle \top, \delta \rangle
    \end{equation}

    Let $\mathbb{E}$ be the space of Events and respectively,
    let $\mathbb{D}$ is the space of possible discernibles $\delta$.

\end{formalism}

Notably, with this point, the faculty of a {\it Link}, a connection, between
two discernibles is established.

The discernible $\delta_\dot$ provides identity to the event---the Identity of
being not the Noumena. It establishes the faculty of {\it Differentiation}. The
only true information content possible at this initial moment must be about
the only existing entity, i.e.\ the Noumena. It is the Noumena's information of
``I exist''. Due to the inability to interact with any other entity in the
nascent world of conceptions, $e_\dot$ is unable to develop any type of
dynamic. It is immediately the first converged \textbf{Fixed Point}. It
constitutes the first instance of immutability, stability, and thus, the first
moment of \textbf{Fixation}. It becomes an {\it Atomic Fact}.

\begin{formalism}[Atomic Fact]

    An \textbf{Atomic Fact} $a \in \mathbb{F}$ is the immutable trace of
    an Event. An atomic fact is the result of a fixation, where no logical
    implication leads to further events.

    An event $e = \langle \top, \delta \rangle$ transitions into an atomic fact:

    \begin{equation}
        a := \langle \bot, \delta \rangle
    \end{equation}

    The formation of an Atomic Fact corresponds to the ontological shift from
    the truth of \textit{Becoming} ($\top$) to the truth of \textit{Has-Become}
    ($\bot$). The information content, the identity provider $\delta$, remains
    intact.

    \begin{equation}
        \langle \top, \delta \rangle \rightarrow \langle \bot, \delta \rangle
    \end{equation}

    Let $\mathbb{F}$ be the Universe of Facts.

\end{formalism}

For the sake of clarity, $\top$ is the indubitable truth of an event that it is
an event. $\bot$ is the truth of a discernible to have passed into fixation.
With Events and Atomic Facts, we identify two domains, defined as follows:

\begin{formalism}[Synthetic Interstice and Immutable Past]

    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Synthetic Interstice ($\sigma$):} A domain where there is
        no time. Events instantiate and imply further events instantaneously 
        until they converge under the rules of consistency. Anything that happens 
        in the Synthetic Interstice is indubitably true but possesses no temporal
        extent.
        \item \textbf{Immutable Past ($H$):} The domain of converged, fixated 
        sets of events. Content of the Immutable Past exists for ever.
    \end{itemize}

\end{formalism}

Still in a void of anything that may establish any concept of time, the fixated
content $\delta_\dot$ of the Noumena's ``I exist'' becomes an immutable atomic
fact, thus implying an event with the information of ``I exist permanently''.

Notably, with the first logical convergence of $\delta_\dot$---with the first
fixation---two modes of truth emerge: the ephemeral truth of $\top$, the
\textit{Becoming}, and the immutable truth of $\bot$, the truth modality of
\textit{Have-Become}. With logical implications mapping from the immutable past
into the dynamic present ($\bot \rightarrow \top$), \textit{Sequentiality}
is established. 

\begin{formalism}[Sequentiality]
    Sequentiality within the Immutable Past is established strictly through the 
    logical implications that referenced the Immutable Past during the generative 
    operation of the Synthetic Interstice. 
    
    That is, if an Atomic Fact $a_1$, produced in a Synthetic Interstice $\sigma_1$, 
    was necessitated by an antecedent Atomic Fact $a_0$ from a prior interstice 
    $\sigma_0$, they possess a strict structural order: $a_1 \succ a_0$ ($a_1$ after $a_0$).
\end{formalism}

Where the first Synthetic Interstice originated in a pure conceptual vacuum,
any further Synthetic Interstice exists as a consequence, a successor,
operating strictly independently of its predecessor. It operates solely on its
current dynamic events and the formal implications of the Immutable Past.

Information only exists if it informs. If an observer already possesses specific
information content, any new arrival carrying this identical content cannot
inform the observer and, therefore, is no information to him. By necessity,
the existence of information $\delta_\dot$ implies the existence of a {\it Lack
of Knowledge}, i.e.\ an entity that can be informed, namely a {\it Cognitive
Subject}. 

Furthermore, the same information cannot exist twice synchronously, as the first
instance of information would take away the property of informing from the second
instance. Thus, in this primordial engine, redundancy collapses instantly. The
structural prevention of redundancy necessitates, and therefore proves, the
existence of the faculties of {\it Equivalence\/} and {\it Differentiation\/} at
this early moment.

Once events are experienced by the Cognitive Subject, the corresponding information
ceases to exist as an active signal---it does not inform anymore. Its content
becomes immutable. This establishes the event $e_{:}$ carrying the information
$\delta_{:}$ of the Noumena's ``I instantiate/inform''. This is another fixed
point in the evolution. The Synthetic Interstice is the sole source of experience
for the Cognitive Subject. In order to perceive the Immutable Past, the Subject
must be able to generate Events in the Synthetic Interstice \textit{from} it. As
long as it lacks this faculty, its perception remains dormant at this point of
evolution. 

\begin{formalism}[Memory]
    Let \textbf{Memory} refer to the faculty of the Cognitive Subject to
    instantiate events in the Synthetic Interstice based on the traces
    in the Immutable Past. This projection of an Immutable Past into the 
    Synthetic Interstice allows the Cognitive Subject to `see' elements of the
    Immutable Past. 
\end{formalism}

Memory, therefore, marks the second stage---after being instantiated as a lack
of knowledge---in the existence of the Cognitive Subject. 

The Immutable Past is the substance that possesses the required immutability to
constitute the material of the experienced \textbf{Universe}. The Immutable
Past is the information sedimented from an event with a truth modality of
$\top$ ({\it Becoming\/}) into an Atomic Fact with a truth modality of $\bot$
({\it Have-Become\/}). Memory is the faculty to instantiate events from Atomic
Facts, i.e.\ to produce $\top$ from $\bot$. This establishes the faculty of
{\it Implication\/} from the Immutable Past into the present, the Synthetic
Interstice. 

With the faculty of Memory, the Cognitive Subject can experience the Immutable
Past. The fixated $\delta_\dot$ ``I exist'' and $\delta_{:}$ ``I instantiate''
imply a third event $e_{\circlearrowright}$ with a discernible
$\delta_{\circlearrowright}$ ``I instantiate permanently''. This ignites the
engine of evolution for the Cognitive Subject's universe. The engine operates on
consistency---consistency with Truth, which has been provided by the Noumena.
This implies the event $e_{\underline{\bullet}}$ carrying the information of the
Noumena's ``I sustain this universe''. Precisely, $\delta_{\underline{\bullet}}$
informs about the established transitions of truth modalities:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Noumena:} instantiates $\top$.
    \item \textbf{Implications within Synthetic Interstice:} instantiate $\top \rightarrow \top$.
    \item \textbf{Implications from Immutable Past (Memory):} instantiate $\bot \rightarrow \top$.
    \item \textbf{Synthetic Interstice Convergence:} operates $\top \rightarrow \bot$.
\end{itemize}

Let us summarize what we have established at this stage of the cosmogony. We have
discernibles $\delta_\dot$, $\delta_{:}$, $\delta_\circlearrowright$, and
$\delta_{\underline{\bullet}}$, given as absolute information about the Noumena
itself, namely:

\begin{itemize}
    \item[$\delta_\dot$:]                ``I exist''.
    \item[$\delta_{:}$:]                 ``I instantiate''.
    \item[$\delta_\circlearrowright$:]   ``I exist and instantiate permanently''.
    \item[$\delta_{\underline{\bullet}}$:] ``I sustain the universe''.
\end{itemize}

There currently exists the concepts of information and information content, the
concepts of {\it Events\/} and {\it Atomic Facts}, {\it Differentiation\/} and
{\it Equivalence}, {\it Instantiation\/} and {\it Fixation}, {\it Immutability},
the {\it Synthetic Interstice\/} with events of truth modality $\top$, the {\it

Subject}. There exists an engine, driven on consistency beyond time, that
receives events with a truth modality $\top$, operates $\top \rightarrow \top$

In the following, we designate this {\it metaphysical engine\/} as

\begin{formalism}[The Manifold]

    The \textbf{Manifold} $\mathcal{M}$ is the transcendental engine that
    converts Ontological Truth (Noumena) into Epistemic Truth (World). It is

    \begin{equation}
        \mathcal{M} := \langle \sigma, H, \Phi \rangle
    \end{equation}

    \begin{description}
        \item[$\sigma$ (The Synthetic Interstice):] The domain of pure dynamic

        \item[$H$ (The Immutable Past):] The domain of pure static, immutable

    \end{description}

\end{formalism}

Since the Noumena is strictly un-modeled and un-restricted, it necessarily

\section{Metaphysics}

A fundamental premise of this inquiry is that the dynamic reality underlying
the perceived universe is generated by logical operations on a \textbf{Metaphysical
Substance}. Unlike physical matter, this substance is neither constrained by
locality nor bound by the consumption of energy. It is the fabric of
\textit{Validity} itself.

This reality is evidenced by the behavior of logical properties, which function
strictly independently of physical constraints. We will formally establish the
operators of \texttt{AND}, \texttt{OR}, and \textit{Set} based on primordial
faculties in subsequent sections. However, we invoke them here to demonstrate
the fundamental nature of the Manifold.

Consider two distinct entities, $A$ and $B$, situated at opposite edges of a
theoretical physical horizon. Let $P$ be a shared predicate such that $P(A)$
is true and $P(B)$ is true. We define the set $\mathcal{X} = \{ e \mid P(e) \}$.
Currently, the cardinality $|\mathcal{X}| \ge 2$.

Consequently, $B$ holds the relational property of being \textit{Co-Existent}
in $\mathcal{X}$. If $A$ ceases to hold property $P$ (or ceases to exist), the
truth value of the proposition ``$B$ is one of a pair in $\mathcal{X}$''
changes to \textbf{False} \textit{instantaneously}. The logical status of $B$
is altered without any physical signal traversing the space between $A$ and
$B$.

The same holds for the Boolean operation $\top(A \land B)$. If $A$ transitions
to False, the aggregate truth of the conjunction collapses to False
instantaneously, regardless of $B$'s state. Thus, we conclude:

\begin{quote}
    \textbf{Implication, Conjunction, and Relation operate on properties
    without the consumption of energy and without physical constraints 
    such as the speed of light.}
\end{quote}

To dismiss the ontological reality of this logical bond as a mere ``mental
construct'' is to undermine the foundation of mathematical realism. If Set
Membership, Equality, or Implication do not possess ontological validity
independent of the observer's momentary thought, then the capacity of
mathematics to describe the physical universe becomes inexplicable~\cite{Wigner1960}.
It would imply that the universe has no inherent logical structure---rendering
scientific inquiry itself impossible.

We therefore postulate that any Cognitive Subject is necessarily operating
within a \textbf{Conceptualized Reality}, generated by the Manifold. The
Ground Reality (Noumena) exists independently of these conceptions, but the
laws of Logic (The Laws of the Manifold) are the governing dynamics of the
Subject's world. This postulate is \textbf{logically prior} to any purely
physicalist model, for physics itself is a discipline entirely contained within
the world of conceptions. Consequently, this inquiry constitutes the
\textbf{Transcendental Condition} of the possibility of physics itself.

\subsection{The Object and the Set}

The following section establishes a rigorous ontological demarcation between an
{\it Object}, defined as a static composition of co-existing discernibles,
and a {\it Set}, defined as the dynamic, generative structure required for
evolution across logical steps. Their fundamental dichotomy in nature is an
absolute prerequisite for navigating the mechanics of the Manifold in the
subsequent discussion.

Let the term {\it Object\/} denote a redundancy free composition, i.e.\
co-existing discernibles with a truth modality of $\top$ or $\bot$. An object
is the result of a implication that multiple events co-exist.

\begin{formalism}[Object]
    An \textit{Object} is a configuration of discernibles that is instantiated
    by the implication of their co-existence. 

    A \textit{Nascent Object} is an object with a truth modality of $\top$
    in the Synthetic Interstice. It has no temporal expansion. An \textit{Object}
    (without the predicate nascent) is the fixation of a Nascent Object
    in the Immutable Past.
\end{formalism}

Notably, the discernibles of an Nascent Object have no other condition that
ties them together other than co-existence, their shared truth modality $\top$
and $\bot$ at the logical moment when their events already exist. The
primordial framework, at this point, can establish objects without restriction
or contradiction. An object can be seen as a `snapshot of discernibles' that
have been implied at a logical step of implications.

For the Synthetic Interstice, though, we need a Set definition that behaves
robust in the dynamic evolution of logical steps. Also, in general,
the core characteristic of a \textit{Set} is not restricted to the truth modality.
Our definition of a Set is based in the set-theoretic
foundations of Cantor~\cite{Cantor1895} and the logical principles of
Leibniz~\cite{Leibniz1714}. A set, in this sense, is not a `snapshot of a
fenced number of elements'. Rather, its existence is bound to a generative
logical process.

\begin{enumerate}

    \item A \textit{Virtual Seed} $s_0$ possessing a specific $\delta_0$
          \textit{core characteristic}---and no other discernible $\delta$. 

          \textit{Ontological Precedence} dictates that the \textit{Virtual Seed} 
          cannot rely on predicates that are not yet established.

    \item A \textit{One-More} operation. This operation is established by 
          \textit{Equivalence} of a perceived object $s_i$ with respect 
          to the core characteristic of $\delta_0$, and which exhibits 
          \textit{Differentiation} via at least one secondary characteristic 
          $\delta$ (discernible) from any other object $s_\omega$ already in 
          the set.

    \item A set is defined as the fixed point where the number of eligible 
          objects is exhausted.  

\end{enumerate}

The ontological faculty to of `convergence' was established by
the very first instantiation from the Noumena---the Primordial Event
$e_\bullet$. As an isolated entity in a conceptual void, its generative process
lacked any valid antecedents for a `One-More' operation---nothing else exists
about what one could inform. Therefore, its generative set exhausted
instantaneously at a cardinality of one. That primordial exhaustion established
the foundational faculty of \textbf{Fixation}. The convergence of any
subsequent, complex set in the Synthetic Interstice is simply the application 
of this original faculty.

This generative definition structurally prevents Russell's
\textbf{antinomy}~\cite{Russell} of the ``set of all sets that do not contain
themselves as an element''. This particular set would require the definition of
a virtual seed $s_0$ as ``does not contain itself''. This predicate, however,
is a reference to something that is not yet ontologically established, namely
the set itself. The antinomy cannot arise, because Russell's set is not a set
at all in the aforementioned sense.

We established the concept of a \textit{Set} in terms of \textit{Equivalence},
\textit{Differentiation}, and \textit{Implication}--all present in the
Synthetic Interstice. Further, this definition of a set relies on purely
dynamic generation rather than on a `fixation' (as a `snapshot of member
elements'). This independence from `fixation' is crucial for the operation of
the Synthetic Interstice. With the already established concept of
Sequentiality, we and the definition of a Set, we are now able to precisely
define the Immutable Past.

\begin{formalism}[Immutable Past]

    \textbf{Immutable Past} $H_k$ is the immutable, totally ordered sequence 
    of sets of Atomic Facts recorded up to logical step $k$.

    \begin{equation}
        H_k := \langle \mathit{F}(N^0_0), \mathit{F}(N^0_1), \dots, \mathit{F}(N^0_{k-1}) \rangle
    \end{equation}

    The Immutable Past is the cumulative result of fixation upon the completion
    of the Synthetic Interstice.

\end{formalism}

While Past is relative to the Cognitive Subject, it is not arbitrary. It
constitutes the \textbf{Phenomenal Objectivity} of the world—tangible,
unchangeable, and a basis for all implications of future Becoming.

\subsection{Negation}

A critical distinction must be made regarding the concept of ``Negation'', namely 
the between the faculties of \textbf{Inequality} and \textbf{Complementation}.

\begin{formalism}[Inequality (The Negation of Identity)]
    Inequality is the characteristic of \textbf{Differentiation} between two positively 
    existing entities. It relies on the existence of a discerning attribute $delta$
    of both. 
    
    \begin{equation}
        A \neq B \iff \Big( \exists \delta_{a,i} \in \delta(A) \mid \nexists \delta_{b,k} \in \delta(B) : \delta_{a,i} = \delta_{b,k} \Big) \lor \Big( \exists \delta_{b,k} \in \delta(B) \mid \nexists \delta_{a,i} \in \delta(A) : \delta_{b,k} = \delta_{a,i} \Big)
    \end{equation}

    That is, for $A$ to be unequal to $B$ there must be either a discernible 
    in $A$ which is not in $B$ or vice versa.

\end{formalism}

\begin{formalism}[Complementation (The Negation of Exclusion)]
    Complementation is the logical construction of a ``Not-Concept''. It is
    defined as the set of all elements in a specific Universe $U$ that are not
    the target element $e$.
    
    \begin{equation}
        \neg e := U \setminus \{e\}
    \end{equation}
    
    Complementation can only be established if and only if $U$ is a
    \textbf{Bounded Set}. One cannot subtract from an undefined totality.
\end{formalism}

We posit that the set of potentially arising events $\mathbb{D}$, stemming
directly from the Noumena, corresponds to what Cantor termed the
\textbf{Absolute Infinite} ($\Omega$)~\cite{Cantor1899}. Because the Noumena is
the `most potent source of reality', strictly un-modeled and un-limited, it
cannot be encapsulated into a single, bounded container. The set of possible
events $\mathbb{D}$ is a {\it Proper Class\/} according Von
Neumann–Bernays–Gödel (NBG) set theory~\cite{VonNeumann1925}.

Since $\mathbb{D}$ is unbounded, it cannot establish a Universe $U$ required
for the complement operation. Consequently, we derive the following structural
laws:

\begin{formalism}[The Constraint of Positivity]
    \textbf{The Synthetic Interstice ($\sigma$):} 
    Since the domain of events $\mathbb{E}$ is a Proper Class, there is no valid 
    universe $U$ to define $\neg e$. Therefore, the Synthetic Interstice operates 
    under \textbf{Strict Positivity}.
    \begin{equation}
        \nexists (\neg e) \in \sigma
    \end{equation}
    
    \textbf{Immutable Past ($H_k$):} 
    Past is, by definition, the \textit{fixation} of a specific, saturated set 
    of events $N_k$. It is bounded and finite (or countable). Therefore, $H_k$ 
    forms a valid Universe, allowing for the operation of Complementation.
    \begin{equation}
        \exists (\neg a) \iff a \in H_k
    \end{equation}
\end{formalism}

This asymmetry implies a profound metaphysical truth: The Cognitive Subject can
only experience ``what is not'' by looking backwards at what has already
become. The Future and the Now are purely positive, only perceiving the past
allows the experience of `negation'.

\section{The Synthetic Interstice}

The aforementioned set definition allows operations of the Synthetic Interstice
to operate freely, without producing contradictions. With this introduction, we
can formalize the operational domain.

\begin{formalism}[The Synthetic Interstice]

The \textbf{Synthetic Interstice} $\sigma_k$ is the entity that operates on
logical rules on information between the un-conceptualizable Noumena and
the Immutable Past, that is tangible for the Cognitive Subject. It is the
domain of \textit{Completing}. A $sigma_k$ and a $\sigma_{k+1}$ can be
ordered, by the sequence of their produced fixed points. However, each
$\sigma_k$ operates in a domain with a zero temporal expand. A Synthetic
Interstice ends  is defined by the iterative, monotonic saturation of the
set $N_k$ via an Implication Set $\mathcal{I}$:

\begin{equation}
    \sigma_k : N^{(i)} \xrightarrow{\mathcal{I}} N^{(i+1)} \quad \text{until} \quad N^{(i+1)} \equiv N^{(i)}
\end{equation}

When this point is reached, the saturation $N^{(i)}$ defines the fixed point $N^0_k$.

A Synthetic Interstice is characteristic by:
\begin{itemize}

    \item \textbf{Instantaneous Ephemerality:} It instantiates ephemeral 
       events ($e \in \mathbb{E}$) that possess Existence ($\top$) but are
       not yet fixated into the Immutable Past ($H_k$).

    \item \textbf{Positivity:} In the Synthetic Interstice, events instantiate 
        but never ``un-exist''. The generative logic strictly relies on the 
        presence of Events ($\top$) and the potential absence of Atomic Facts ($\bot$).

    \item \textbf{Absence of Redundancy:} Information that would exist twice
       instantaneously collapses into a single entity of information. That
       is, the co-existence $\delta \oplus \delta$ collapses into $\delta$.

    \item \textbf{Dynamic Logical Construct:} It applies active Events as
       non-negatable antecedents in Implications ($\top \rightarrow \top$).

    \item \textbf{Historic Logical Construct:} It applies the Immutable Past ($H_k$)
       as a valid antecedent in Implications ($\bot \rightarrow \top$).

    \item \textbf{Convergence:} The interstice persists until the application
       of $\mathcal{I}$ reaches a fixed point of saturation ($N^0_k$). This 
       exhaustion of the generative set subsequently triggers Fixation ($\top \rightarrow \bot$).

\end{itemize}
\end{formalism}

Crucially, the elements populating this generative set are the \textbf{Events}
established in the Genesis section. Each element $e \in N^{(i)}$ carries the
tuple $\langle \top, \delta \rangle$. The \textit{Virtual Seed} of the
Interstice is the core predicate of current validity ($\top$). The \textit{One-More} 
operation is the instantiation of a new event that satisfies the implication
