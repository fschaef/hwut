\begin{abstract}

Human interaction with the environment---whether through perception, discourse,
or physical manipulation---is fundamentally the processing of information through
conceptual architectures. The objects of the perceivable universe do not possess
immediate ontological reality as bare physical matter; rather, they are structured
experiences instantiated within a \textit{substance of information}. Consequently,
any rigorous inquiry into the origin of reality cannot commence with physical
models (such as the Big Bang), as physics itself is entirely a downstream
cognitive construct. 

Based on this epistemic demarcation, this text proposes a formal metaphysical
framework for the cosmogony of the Conceptual Universe. Starting from an
un-conceptualizable Absolute (the Noumena) and the primordial instantiation of
an informational Event, we trace the logical evolution of reality for a Cognitive
Subject. By strictly enforcing an \textit{Absence of Redundancy} within a
generative logical engine (the `Ontic Engine`), we formally derive the transcendental
conditions of experience. We demonstrate that the discreteness of time, the
emergence of magnitude, the formulation of causal laws, and the birth of conscious
Agency (the Will) are not Ground-Truth physical phenomena, but necessary structural
consequences of pure logical operations executed upon the substance of information.

\end{abstract}

\section{Foundation}

This text considers the \textit{Physical Universe} strictly as a
\textit{Conceptual Experience} of a \textit{Cognitive Subject}. Physics is a
discipline of models, and models are conceptual architectures. Therefore, an
inquiry into the origin of reality cannot commence with physical time, space,
or matter (e.g., the Big Bang), as these concepts inherently presuppose a
cognitive framework to be intelligible. All subjects of physics are models, and
by necessity, are downstream constructs of cognitive processes. Models live in
minds and are not to be confused with the ground reality that produces them.
Based on these premises, we focus our cosmogony on the evolution of conceptual
experiences.

We posit that at the origin of everything, only Absolute Truth exists as an
un-modeled, un-conceptualizable reality. Let this entity be called the
\textit{Noumena} following Kant~\cite{Kant1781}. In the absence of
conceptuality, there are no conceptualizable answers to \textit{How} or
\textit{Why} the Noumena exists, because conceptual reasoning is restricted to
the world of conceptions. The Noumena exists beyond. Furthermore, there is no
\textit{Separation}, no \textit{Categorization}, and no \textit{Multitude}
within the Noumena, as \textit{Plurality} requires \textit{Differentiation},
and \textit{Differentiation} requires concepts as a basis for comparison.

The Noumena is not a ``gap-filler'' for the yet-unexplained, but rather the
absolute boundary condition of human cognition. While it cannot be understood
or grasped by means of concepts, the Noumena is fundamentally not
\textit{Nothingness}. 

Nothingness is the absolute absence of anything. This absolute absence would
necessarily include the absence of any capacity to \textit{instantiate}---that
is, the primordial capacity to bring an entity into existence. Nothingness cannot
instantiate the basis of existence; it cannot instantiate logic, time, physical
laws, nor the manifestation of matter and energy. The Noumena, conversely, is
the un-modeled \textit{Ground Reality} from which the absolute instantiation of
existence inevitably springs.

The Noumena, together with three further concepts---namely \textit{Information
Substance}, \textit{Concept}, and the \textit{Receptivity} of a
\textit{Cognitive Subject}---form the basis of this investigation. 

\begin{formalism}[Ontological Demarcation]

    \begin{description}
        \item[Ground Reality (The Noumena):] The un-modeled, absolute 
        Ground Truth of existence beyond any conceptual understanding.

        \item[Information Substance:] A raw, pre-conceptual form of truth
        that exists independently of an observer as a pure 
        \textit{potential to inform}.

        \item[Concept:] The structural `image' of an Information Substance that
        renders its truth tangible and intelligible to a Cognitive Subject. 
    \end{description}
\end{formalism}

It may seem counterintuitive that we use names like `Noumena' and `Information
Substance'---which are inherently concepts---to circumscribe the nature of
entities that exist beyond conceptualization. Thus, any statement made herein
pertains strictly to the \textit{Shadow} that the process of their manifestation
casts upon our conceptual understanding. We aim to explore how far analyzing
these shadows might grant us \textit{insight} into the true nature of the
universe that surrounds us.

This text proposes a mathematical framework for describing the cosmogony of a
Universe, understood strictly as the conceptual experience of a Cognitive
Subject, driven by the Information Substance originating from the Noumena. We
aim to identify the formal logical operations at the heart of our perception
of the Universe---operations which fundamentally evade physical observation.
This cosmogony is developed strictly through formal logical necessities and
their absolute consistency.

\section{The Primordial Event}

In the primordial void, no time, no dynamic, no physical law, no convergence,
and nothing of conceptual nature exists---only the Noumena. Then, strictly
conditioned by the Noumena---yet through an instantiation devoid of any
conceptualizable \textit{How} or \textit{Why}---a formal foundation of
conceptual existence must have manifested. Let this conceptual existence
be called an \textit{information substance}. It is the manifestation 
of \textit{Differentiation} from the in-itself indifferent \textit{Equivalence} 
of the absolute Ground Truth, namely the Noumena.

Let this entity of conceptual nature that emerges, existing still strictly
beyond time, be defined as \textit{Event}.

\begin{formalism}[Event and Discernible]

    An \textbf{Event} $e$ is the primordial instantiation of a substance of
    information. It possesses no temporal expansion. 

    An Event consists of two linked properties: the absolute truth of its own
    existence $\top$, and a \textit{discernible} $\delta$, which acts as the
    informational substance by means of which it possesses \textit{Identity}.

    \begin{equation}
        e := \langle \top, \delta \rangle
    \end{equation}

\end{formalism}

The term `substance of information' is chosen to express a truth that may
potentially `inform', but exists independently of an entity to be informed.
The Event establishes a \textit{Link} between its two defining properties:
the mode of truth $\top$ and the content $\delta$.

Let $e_\bullet$ be the first event to occur and $\delta_\bullet$ be its
discernible. This discernible provides identity to the event---the identity
of being separate from the Noumena. It establishes \textit{Differentiation}.

At this initial moment, the only valid information content must pertain to the
sole entity: the Noumena. Thus, $\delta_\bullet$ is the conceptualization of
existence (the first concept) relating to the Noumena. 

\begin{formalism}[The Primordial Discernible $\delta_\bullet$]
    Let ``I'' be a logical placeholder for the Identity of the absolute Ground
    Truth beyond conceptualization---the Noumena---in its complete isolation. Let 
    ``exist'' be an expression of its being absolutely real. Then, the first
    informational substance to exist is $\delta_\bullet$, namely:

    \begin{equation}
         \delta_\bullet := \text{``I exist''}
    \end{equation}
\end{formalism}

As the sole existing Event, $e_\bullet$ lacks any counterparts for logical
interaction within the nascent conceptual space. It cannot develop any dynamic.
Therefore, it is immediately a \textbf{Fixed Point} constituting the first
instance of immutability and logical stability. It becomes an \textit{Atomic
Fact}.

\begin{formalism}[Atomic Fact]

    An \textbf{Atomic Fact} $a$ is the immutable trace of an Event. An atomic
    fact is the result of a fixation, where no logical dynamic leads to further
    events.

    An event $e := \langle \top, \delta \rangle$ transitions into an atomic fact:

    \begin{equation}
        a := \langle \bot, \delta \rangle
    \end{equation}
\end{formalism}

The formation of an Atomic Fact corresponds to the ontological shift from
the indubitable truth of \textit{Becoming} ($\top$, active existence) to
the truth of \textit{Having-Become} ($\bot$, passed into inert fixation). The
information content, the identity provider $\delta$, remains intact.

\begin{equation}
    \langle \top, \delta \rangle \rightarrow \langle \bot, \delta \rangle
\end{equation}

With the shift in modality emerges a shift in meaning. When $\delta_\bullet$
``I exist'' shifts from $\top$ to $\bot$, it induces a new entity of
information substance back into $\top$, namely ``I exist immutably/permanently''.
The shift from $\top$ to $\bot$ acted as a generative force---the first
instance of a primordial \textit{Implication}.

Any application of an implication drawn from the Immutable Past into the
logical domain of active convergence ($\bot \rightarrow \top$) leaves a trace
of informational substance. This implies that the current step of convergence
is structurally dependent upon what has already converged and sedimented in the
Immutable Past. This dependency establishes \textit{Sequentiality}.

\begin{formalism}[Sequentiality]
    If an Event $e_2$ in the Synthetic Interstice instantiates as a logical 
    implication of an Atomic Fact $a_1$, then the sedimented Atomic Fact $a_2$ 
    (derived from $e_2$) is a successor of $a_1$.

    This successor relationship between Atomic Facts establishes a strict structural
    order $a_1 \succ a_0$ for the sedimented facts. Let the faculty that 
    establishes this structural order be designated as \textbf{Sequentiality}.
\end{formalism}

By defining $\top$ as the indubitable truth of existence and $\bot$ as the
truth of having passed into fixation, we can identify two foundational
ontological domains:

\begin{formalism}[Synthetic Interstice and Immutable Past]

    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Synthetic Interstice} ($\sigma$): 
 
        The habitat of Events $e \in \mathbb{E}$. The atemporal domain of 
        purely logical convergence. It is the zero-time instant of \textit{Becoming}.
        
        \item \textbf{Immutable Past} ($H$): 

        The habitat of Atomic Facts $a \in \mathbb{A}$. The sequentially structured
        domain of ontologically irrevocable informational substance. The domain
        of \textit{Having-Become}.

        $H_k$ is the immutable, totally ordered sequence of sets of Atomic Facts 
        recorded up to logical step $k$.

        \begin{equation}
            H_k := \langle \mathit{F}(N^0_0), \mathit{F}(N^0_1), \cdots, \mathit{F}(N^0_{k-1}) \rangle
        \end{equation}

        The Immutable Past is the cumulative result of fixation upon the completion
        of the Synthetic Interstice.
    \end{itemize}

\end{formalism}

The progression from one Synthetic Interstice $\sigma_k$ to the next
$\sigma_{k+1}$ does not occur by means of temporal separation, but strictly
through \textbf{Logical Dependency}. The Immutable Past constitutes the totality
of logically converged Fixed Points. While instantiations originating from the
Noumena exist fundamentally beyond time, they enter the conceptual domain in a
logical sequence---manifesting precisely when the informational substance as a
whole achieves the structural capacity to capture them.

The sedimentation of $\delta_\bullet$ into the Immutable Past establishes a
structural contrast to the Synthetic Interstice $\sigma_0$ where it
was originally instantiated. This contrast inherently implies a new truth
within the subsequent Synthetic Interstice $\sigma_1$, namely the Noumena's
``I instantiate'' ($\langle \top, \delta_{:} \rangle$). The static must
sediment before the dynamic can be perceived; thus, ``I exist'' strictly
precedes ``I instantiate''.

Where the first Synthetic Interstice originated in a pure conceptual vacuum,
any subsequent Synthetic Interstice exists as a structural consequence---a
logical successor---without instantenous logical link to its predecessor. It
executes solely upon its current dynamic events ($\top$) and the formal
implications drawn from the sediment of the Immutable Past ($\bot$).

Finally, with the Synthetic Interstice and the Immutable Past established, the 
capacity of potential Events $\mathbb{E}$, the potential discernibles
$\mathbb{D}$ and potential atomic facts $\mathbb{A}$ is established as 
unbounded proper classes.

\section{Metaphysics}


A fundamental premise of this inquiry is that a Cognitive Subject experiences
the universe strictly in terms of informational substance and concepts. The
Subject possesses no direct access to reality. Consequently, what is
phenomenologically perceived as the `tangibility' or `solidity' of an object is
not a material property, but merely the stability of experienced information.

The physical universe and its laws are therefore strictly downstream products
of received information. They are not stability providers, but merely traces
of a stability provided by a reality beyond our conceptual understanding: the
Noumena. Objectivity therefore demands that any investigation of cosmogony be
strictly liberated from attempts to achieve consistency with physical laws.

In particular, space, time, energy, and mass are solely shadows of the
informational substance projected upon the canvas of our conceptual experience.
The ontic engine that produces them, therefore, must operate
independently of physical locality and energy. This reality is evidenced by the
behavior of logical properties, which function strictly independently of
physical constraints. We will formally establish the operators of \texttt{AND},
\texttt{OR}, and \textit{Composition} or \textit{Set} based on primordial
faculties in subsequent sections. However, we invoke them here to demonstrate
the fundamental nature of the engine.

Consider two distinct entities, $A$ and $B$, situated at opposite edges of a
theoretical physical horizon. Let $P$ be a shared predicate such that $P(A)$
is true and $P(B)$ is true. We define the composition $\mathcal{X} = \{ e \mid P(e) \}$.
Currently, the cardinality $|\mathcal{X}| \ge 2$.

Consequently, $B$ holds the relational property of being \textit{Co-Existent}
in $\mathcal{X}$. If $A$ ceases to hold property $P$ (or ceases to exist), the
truth value of the proposition ``$B$ is one of a pair in $\mathcal{X}$''
changes to \textbf{False} \textit{instantaneously}. The logical status of $B$
is altered without any physical signal traversing the space between $A$ and
$B$.

The same holds for the logical conjunction $(A \land B)$. If $A$ transitions
to False, the aggregate truth of the conjunction collapses to False
instantaneously, regardless of $B$'s state. Thus, we conclude:

\begin{quote}
    \textbf{Implication, Conjunction, and Relation operate on properties
    without the consumption of energy and without physical constraints 
    such as the speed of light.}
\end{quote}

To dismiss the ontological reality of this logical bond as a mere ``mental
construct'' is to undermine the foundation of mathematical realism. If Set
Membership, Equality, or Implication do not possess ontological validity
independent of the observer's momentary thought, then the capacity of
mathematics to describe the physical universe becomes inexplicable~\cite{Wigner1960}.
It would imply that the universe has no inherent logical structure---rendering
scientific inquiry itself impossible.

We therefore postulate a strict ontological hierarchy. The Ground Reality
(Noumena) exists absolutely independent of any conceptuality. From it
instantiates the \textbf{Substance of Information}, which is governed strictly
by the non-local, energy-free laws of Logic. This substance sediments, to be
potentially perceived as a conceptualized stable reality by a Cognitive
Subject. This postulate is \textbf{logically prior} to any purely physicalist
model, for physics itself is a discipline entirely contained within the
perceived world of conceptions. Consequently, this inquiry into the operations
of the informational substance constitutes the \textbf{Transcendental
Condition} of the possibility of physics itself.

\subsection{Absence of Redundancy}

The ontological status of information is fundamentally bound to its capacity to 
inform. 

If an observer already possesses specific information content, any new arrival
carrying identical content cannot inform the observer and, therefore, ceases to
be information. By necessity, the existence of primordial information
($\delta_\bullet$) implies a corresponding potential \textit{Lack of
Knowledge}---an entity capable of being informed, namely the \textit{Cognitive
Subject}.

Furthermore, identical information cannot exist twice synchronously, as the 
presence of the first instance inherently negates the informing property of 
the second. Thus, in this primordial engine, redundancy collapses instantaneously. 
This structural prevention of redundancy necessitates, and thereby proves, 
the existence of the faculties of \textit{Equivalence} and \textit{Differentiation} 
at this initial stage.

The Synthetic Interstice, therefore, operates strictly under the \textbf{Absence 
of Redundancy}. All discernibles of events are inherently redundancy-free, 
ensuring the engine processes only absolutely \textit{new} information.

Because the Synthetic Interstice operates strictly under the \textbf{Absence of
Redundancy}, conceptual logic necessitates a strict deduction: the Noumena must
be restricted to instantiating solely redundancy-free events. Yet, the Noumena
is proven to exist as the un-modeled, unrestricted Absolute Truth beyond
conceptuality. 

Here, conceptual rigor inevitably derives a conclusion that directly
contradicts Ground Reality. While the Cognitive Subject is able to logically
detect the paradoxon of this conclusion, it possesses no experience of reality
capable of bridging the contradiction. The Subject is not left in ignorance,
but in inconsistency. It is exposed to paradoxes by the very nature of
conceptualization. 

The paradoxon remains stable from inside the conceptual world. Namely, a
statement ``There is no Truth'' to resolve the contradiction, would collapse
instantaneously: If there was no truth, the statement itself could not be True.
Instead, for the statement to be true, it would have to be wrong. Thus, an
attempt to deny the Absolute Truth is logically invalid. The aforementioned
paradoxon becomes a characteristic of wrongness of the conceptual understanding
and the relation between the Cognitive Subject and the Noumena.

\subsection{The Ontic Engine}

Given that the Synthetic Interstice is subject to the instantiation of Events
from the Noumena, the implication of consequent Events, the sedimentation of
Events into Atomic Facts, and the emanant implication of Atomic Facts back into
the active Interstice, the \textbf{Ontic Engine}---which drives the reality of
the informational substance---can be described by the following foundational
transitions of truth modalities:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Instantiation:} $\emptyset \rightarrow \top$ (The spontaneous emergence of novel Events from the Noumena).
    \item \textbf{Dynamic Implication:} $\top \rightarrow \top$ (The generation of consequent Events within the Synthetic Interstice).
    \item \textbf{Emanant Implication:} $\bot \rightarrow \top$ (The structural retrieval of Atomic Facts into active Events).
    \item \textbf{Fixation (Convergence):} $\top \rightarrow \bot$ (The sedimentation of exhausted Events into the Immutable Past).
\end{itemize}

Because the Noumena operates on absolute consistency, the sequential reality of
the Static ($\delta_\bullet$) and the Dynamic ($\delta_{:}$) acting via these
four transitions structurally implies a third truth to be expressed within the
informational substance: $e_{\circlearrowright}$, carrying the discernible
$\delta_{\circlearrowright}$ (``I instantiate permanently''). 

Furthermore, this continuous cycle implies a fourth truth to be expressed as
informational substance: namely, that the cycle itself is maintained upon the
absolute ground of Truth---otherwise, it could not exist. The corresponding
Event $e_{\underline{\bullet}}$ carries the discernible $\delta_{\underline{\bullet}}$
(``I sustain this universe''). 

The cycle of informational substance operates entirely independently of any
active observer. However, for the informational substance to exist even as a
potentiality, it structurally requires the capacity to `inform'. Therefore, it
can only exist alongside the \textit{potential} existence of an observer (the
Lack of Knowledge). We are now able to formalize this complete, self-sustaining
metaphysical architecture: the \textbf{Ontic Engine} ($\mathcal{O}$).

\begin{formalism}[The Ontic Engine]

    The \textbf{Ontic Engine} $\mathcal{O}$ is the transcendental mechanism that

    \begin{equation}

        the saturation of events occurs.
        
        \item[$H$ (The Immutable Past):] The domain of pure static fixation

        \item[$\Phi$ (The Generative Cycle):] The operational function executing

        ($\emptyset \rightarrow \top$), evaluating implications ($\top \rightarrow \top$,
        $\bot \rightarrow \top$), and enforcing absolute convergence ($\top \rightarrow \bot$).
    \end{description}

\end{formalism}

\subsection{Formalism: Compositions and Sets}

A crucial foundation for the following logical arguments is the strict demarcation
between a \textit{Composition} and a \textit{Set}. First, the co-existence of
multiple discernibles establishes an informational substance---a truth expressing
their co-existence. The strict shared core characteristic that ties the elements
of a composition together is solely that their events all carry the $\top$ truth
modality at the exact same logical step. A composition can be considered a passive
`snapshot' of discernibles at one particular logical step of implications. 

This purely extensional definition of aggregation directly mirrors the Axiom of
Extensionality in Zermelo-Fraenkel set theory~\cite{Zermelo1908}, where the
identity of a set is determined solely by its constituent elements. Let $N^{(i)}$
be the maximal Composition carrying the informational substance of co-existence
for \textit{all} events instantiated at step $i$. 

Under the strict \textbf{Absence of Redundancy}, any isolated sub-composition
asserting the mere co-existence of a specific subset (e.g., $\delta_p \oplus
\delta_q$) instantaneously collapses, as its co-existence is already subsumed
within the maximal composition $N^{(i)}$. Consequently, any strict
sub-composition (where $\delta_{co} \neq N^{(i)}$) can only persist as a distinct
information substance, if it acts as an antecedent to an implication. 

To survive the pressure of the Absence of Redundancy, the sub-composition must
inherently imply an event carrying the truth of $\delta_{\mathcal{I}}$,
namely ``has an implication that relies on the co-existence of the events''.
We therefore formally define the Composition under this strict existential condition:

\begin{formalism}[Composition Discernible]

    A \textbf{Composition Discernible} $\delta_{co,i}$, or simply
    \textit{Composition}, is an informational substance expressing the
    co-existence of multiple distinct discernibles $\delta_0, \delta_1, \ldots$ 
    at a particular step of implications $i$.

    \begin{equation}
        \delta_{co,i} = ((\oplus, \delta_0, \delta_1, \ldots),  \delta_{\mathcal{I}}) 
    \end{equation}

    where $\delta_{\mathcal{I}}$ carries the information substance, that the 
    implication $\mathcal{I}_{co,i}$ requires the co-existence of 
    $\delta_0, \delta_1, \ldots$ as antecedent.

    $\delta_{co,i}$ is instantiated as the implied event $e_{co} = \langle
    \top, \delta_{co,i} \rangle$ at the logical implication step $i$ when all
    its constituent discernibles co-exist.

step in the same Synthetic Interstice. Like any entity possessing the truth
modality $\top$, the composition-instantiating events $\langle \top,

\end{formalism}

\subsection{Formalism: Negation}

A critical distinction must be made regarding the concept of ``Negation'', namely 
the between the faculties of \textbf{Inequality}, \textbf{Complementation}, and

\subsection{The Birth of Will}

Within the mechanics deduced so far, there is no ontological faculty capable of
initiating an event whose content is not necessarily True. The Noumena
strictly instantiates absolute Truth; the Immutable Past strictly provides
sedimented certainty. Therefore, the only possible origin of an Event that carries
a Causal Assertion must be the Cognitive Subject.


This autonomous instantiation of an Event constitutes the first `activity' of
the Cognitive Subject within the unfolding of its own Universe. It is the first
conceptual entity that cannot be attributed to a passive reception of the
Noumena. Instead, it is an \textbf{Action} originating within the Cognitive
Subject. 

The generation of a Causal Assertion Discernible constitutes the first
experiential trace of the most primitive form of \textbf{Will} originating from
the Subject. 

\begin{formalism}[The Primordial Will]

    The \textbf{Will} ($\Pi$) is the active structural faculty of the Cognitive

        \Pi : \mathbb{A} \implies \langle \top, \langle \varnothing, \delta_{law} \rangle \rangle \in \mathbb{E}
    \end{equation}

    The primordial Will marks the ontological transition of the Subject from a
    passive recipient of Truth to an active instantiator. Each generated Event, 
    though carries $\varnothing$.

\end{formalism}

\subsection{The Hallucination of Future}

With the Synthetic Interstice established as the absolute, active boundary of
instantiation, there is no ontological concept of a `Future' serving as a
counterpart to the Immutable Past. The Future is ontologically void.

However, equipped with Causal Assertions, the Cognitive Subject may hallucinate
a future by means of \textbf{Determinism}---projecting predicted outcomes based
on assumed causal laws. Because nothing conceptual exists beyond the Synthetic
Interstice, these predictions are entirely speculative. This demonstrates how
the discernible of wrongness ($\varnothing$) dissipates outward, fundamentally
coloring the Cognitive Subject's perception of the entire universe with the
illusion of a pre-determined reality.

\section{Conclusion}

-----
If we acknowledge that the Noumena exists prior to any conceptual rules of
logic or structure, it cannot be viewed as a passive entity subjected to
mechanical laws. The spontaneous instantiation of the informational substance
``I exist'' is not the output of a deterministic algorithm, but the
unconditioned expression of Absolute Reality reflecting upon its own existence.

This primordial act of self-relation forces a profound realization: the essence
of \textbf{Consciousness}---defined at its absolute root as the pure awareness
of one's own existence---must originate in the Noumena itself. 

A strict ontological demarcation must therefore be made between this Primordial
Consciousness and the downstream \textit{Cognitive Subject}. As will be
established, the Cognitive Subject requires the evolution of faculties such as
Memory to reflect upon sedimented facts (matter) and to navigate sequential
time. However, the pure awareness of existence ($\delta_\bullet$) requires
neither time nor matter. It is the absolute, unconditioned presence from which
all subsequent conceptual reality flows.
