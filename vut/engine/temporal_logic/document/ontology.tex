\section*{Nomenclature}

To ensure ontological precision, the following symbols are defined as the
invariant vocabulary of the Subject:

\begin{description}[style=multiline, leftmargin=3cm, font=\bfseries]

    \item[$\mathcal{S}$] \textbf{The Subject.} The transcendental entity
        defined by the tuple $\langle \mathcal{M}, \mathcal{I}, \Psi, \Pi
        \rangle$. That is, the manifold $\mathcal{M}$, the implication set $\mathcal{I}$,
        the schema of understanding $\Psi$, and the will $\Pi$ (Proairesis).
    
    \item[$\mathbb{E}$] \textbf{Universe of Discourse.} The set of all possible
        Phenomenal Events.
    
    \item[$\mathbb{A}$] \textbf{Universe of Assertions.} The set of all
        possible Constituted Truths (Statements). With the subspaces 
        $\mathbb{A}^\top$ of evident assertions, $\mathbb{A}^\diamond$ of
        unrefuted assertions, and $\mathbb{A}^\bot$ of refuted 
        assertions. 
    
    \item[$\mathcal{M}$] \textbf{The Manifold.} The logical space where events
        of \textit{The Now} accumulate. 
    
    \item[$N^0_k$] \textbf{The Now.} The saturated set of events existing in the
        Manifold at logical time step $k$. ($N_k \subset \mathbb{E}$).
    
    \item[$\mathcal{I}$] \textbf{The Implication Set.} The set of positive,
        monotonic rules that drive the saturation of the Manifold ($A \implies
        B$).
    
    \item[$\sigma$] \textbf{The Synthetic Interstice.} The
        orthogonal dimension of logical time where $\mathcal{I}$ is applied
        until a fixed point is reached.
    
    \item[$H_k$] \textbf{History.} The immutable sequence of recorded Nows.
        $H_k = \langle N_0, \dots, N_{k-1} \rangle$.
    
    \item[$\Psi$] \textbf{The Schema of Understanding.} The Hermeneutic
        function that maps History to State ($\Psi: H_k \to S_k$).
    
    \item[$S_k$] \textbf{The State.} The set of active Assertions held as true
        by the Subject ($S_k \subset \mathbb{A}$).
    
    \item[$\Pi$] \textbf{The Will (Proairesis).} The function that generates 
        Petitions to the Noumena to cause specific assertions in some future State.

\end{description}

\section{Metaphysics}

A fundamental premise of this text is that the dynamic reality underlying the
perceived universe is generated by logical operations on a metaphysical
substance that is neither constrained by physical locality nor bound by the
consumption of energy.

This reality is evidenced by considering `properties' that concern objects
entangled without the physical constraints of location. Let $A$ and $B$ be two
`objects' for which any kind of common property $x$ can be established. Let
$\mathcal{X}$ be the set of objects with property $x$, consisting solely of
$\{A, B\}$. Then each one, $A$ and $B$, possesses the property of being
\textit{One-of-Two} in $\mathcal{X}$.

If $A$ loses property $x$, then $B$ \textit{instantaneously} loses the property
of being \textit{One-of-Two}, even if $A$ and $B$ are located at opposite edges
of the cosmological horizon. The same holds for the truth of the \texttt{AND}
operation---namely ``$A$ and $B$ have $x$''---which loses its truth
instantaneously. Similarly, the \texttt{OR} operation gains or loses truth
instantaneously, depending on the scenario.\ \textit{Implication}, \texttt{AND},
and \texttt{OR} clearly operate on properties without the consumption of energy
and without the constraints of the speed of light.

To dismiss the ontological reality of a logical bond between $A$ and $B$ as a
mere `mental construct' is to undermine the foundation of mathematical realism.
If set membership or equality do not possess ontological validity independent
of the observer's mind, then the capacity of mathematics to describe the
physical universe becomes inexplicable~\cite{Wigner1960}. It would imply that
the universe has no inherent logical structure---rendering scientific inquiry
itself impossible.

\section{The Noumena and the Cognitive Subject}

We begin with the axiom of \textit{Finite Receptivity}~\cite{Kant1781}. There
is the {\it Cognitive Subject\/} and the {\it Noumena\/}---the latter being the
``Outside'' of the Subject, the unknowable, un-modeled, un-conceptualizable
Absolute Reality. As Kant rigorously demonstrated, the thing-in-itself, the
Noumena, remains forever beyond the grasp of the cognitive subject.

This text proposes a mathematical framework for describing the metaphysical
process underlying a {\it Cognitive Subject\/}'s interaction with absolute
reality. We aim to identify the mathematical mechanics at the heart of our
perception of the universe---mechanics which evade physical observation but
reveal themselves through logical necessity.

In the text, the expression ``faculty of X'' shall serve as a shorthand for the
``ability to experience the existence of X''. We start from a minimum set of
undeniable experiences. The Subject is defined by its exposure to the following
primordial faculties that precede any specific content:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Equivalence:} The faculty to experience a shared 
    characteristic between two distinct entities. 

    \item \textbf{Differentiation:} The faculty to experience a unique 
    characteristic ($\delta$) that distinguishes an entity from another. 

    \item \textbf{Instantiation:} The faculty to experience \textit{Novelty}---the 
    appearance of an entity that has not been experienced before.

    \item \textbf{Necessity (Implication):} The faculty to experience the 
    generation of an entity $B$ purely because $A$ exists. 

    \item \textbf{Immutability (Fixation):} The faculty to experience the 
    resistance of an entity to change. 
\end{enumerate}

\section{The Primordial Event and the Origin of Fixation}

We start from the Noumena: an un-conceptualizable Absolute Truth---a ground
reality---an existence strictly independent of the concepts of a Cognitive
Subject. At this origin point, no fixation, no writing, is assumed to exist;
there is only the initial Instantiation of a \textbf{Primordial Event} $e_0$.

This initial Event holds two predicates:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Truth ($\top$):} The truth of its own existence (Veritas).
    \item \textbf{Discernible ($\delta$):} A characteristic that marks its 
    differentiation from the Absolute Noumena. It is the seed of its own 
    \textbf{Identity}.
\end{enumerate}

Due to the inability to interact with any other entity in the nascent `world of
conceptions', the Primordial Event is unable to develop any type of dynamic. It
is immediately the first converged \textbf{Fixed Point}. It constitutes the
first instance of immutability, stability, and thus, the first moment of
\textbf{Fixation}.

Since no concept of `fixation' existed prior to this moment, the Discernible
predicate of the initial Event acts as the concept of `fixation' itself. The
distinction of the event \textit{is} the act of writing. The Primordial Event
is the `pencil' that generates the concept of an immutable past.

However, the concept of immutability cannot exist in isolation; it requires
contrast through the faculty of \textbf{Differentiation}. The definition of the
\textit{Static} structurally necessitates the definition of the
\textit{Dynamic}.

Consequently, the fixation of the primordial event implies the instantiation of
a second event $e_1$, carrying the discernible of \textbf{The Dynamic}.

\begin{equation}
    \text{Fixation}(e_0) \xrightarrow{\text{Diff}} \exists e_1 : \delta(e_1) = \text{Dynamic}
\end{equation}

With this implication, the Universe expands beyond a singularity. It now
comprises three constituents: the immutable Primordial Past, the active
operation of Fixation, and the newly implied potential of the Dynamic. The
Dynamic necessitates further instantiation of events from the Noumena---out of
the absolute Truth. This defines the primordial cycle of creation, an engine
capable of generating a reality for the Cognitive Subject in its world of
conceptualization.

In the following, we designate this {\it metaphysical engine\/} as
$\mathcal{M}$, the {\it Manifold\/}---adopting Kant's terminology. Let the
domain of pure dynamic that permanently generates new immutable pasts be called
the {\it Synthetic Interstice\/} ($\sigma$). It is necessarily a gap in time,
situated between moments of fixation. Let the permanently generated 
immutable past be called {\it History}. Notably, we derived this engine from a
minimum set of faculties: {\it Equivalence}, {\it Differentiation}, {\it
Necessity}, as well as {\it Instantiation\/} and {\it Immutability}. 

The Manifold comprises two modes of existence: The purely dynamic Synthetic
Interstice beyond any fixation, any beyond any tangibility, the most extreme
form of dynamic,  which can only exist in a gap of time, and the immutable
History, providing tangibility for the cognitive Subject.

In the Synthetic Interstice, ephemeral {\it Events\/} pop into existence. {\it
Original Events\/} are instantiated by the Noumena and {\it Implied Events\/} are
`necessitated' by the rules of nature~\footnote{Here `nature' shall designate the
structure that exists without the creational intervention of the Cognitive
Subject.}. Events are the smallest entity of information that allows for the
perception of {\it Identity}, i.e.\ the smallest unit that allows to
differentiate a $\delta_a$ from a $\delta_b$. Events solely exist in the gap in time, the
Synthetic Interstice---thus have no temporal expansion. 

The Synthetic Interstice, the gap in time, is where any dynamic perceived by
the Cognitive Subject originates from. It is the closest interface to absolute
reality. It is where reality manifests. When its atemporal operations reach a
fixed point, the fixation/writing of the resulting Events marks the end of the
Synthetic Interstice and the beginning of a new. The distinction between the
purely dynamic, non-writing, nature of the Synthetic Interstice and the
written, immutable, nature of History has a significant impact on the
operational logic of the Manifold.

The {\it fixation\/} of events into History marks the end of a Synthetic
Interstice. During the purely dynamic Synthetic Interstice, no `write operations'
happen. The operations during the Synthetic Interstice rely solely on the 
truth of '{\it Becoming\/}'. In contrast, History expresses the truth of `{\it
Has-Become\/}'. Fixation marks the boundary between the dynamic present of
the Synthetic Interstice and the immutable History. This has significant impact
on the logic that may be applied during the Synthetic Interstice.

The following discussion applies a definition of sets which is grounded in

\begin{enumerate}

\end{enumerate}

Notably, we established the concept of a {\it Set\/} in terms of {\it

\section{Inequality and Complementation}

We posit that Negation not as a singular atomic operation. Instead we
distinguish between two constituting faculties: \textbf{Inequality} and
\textbf{Complementation}. 

\begin{formalism}[Inequality (The Negation of Identity)]
    Inequality is the characteristic of \textbf{Distinction} between two positively 
    existing entities. It relies on the existence of a discerning attribute $delta$
    of both. 
    
    \begin{equation}
        A \neq B \iff \delta(A) \neq \delta(B)
    \end{equation}
\end{formalism}

\begin{formalism}[Complementation (The Negation of Exclusion)]
    Complementation is the logical construction of a ``Not-Concept''. 
    Complementation is established through inequality of a candidate
    with any other element in a completed \textbf{Universe} $U$.
    (Notably, we do not use the term subtraction in at this point in time).
    
    \begin{equation}
        \neg e := U \setminus \{e\}
    \end{equation}
    
    Complementation can only be established if and only if $U$ 
    is a defined/bounded \textbf{Set}. 
\end{formalism}

We posit that the set of potentially arising events $\mathbb{E}$, stemming
directly from the Noumena, corresponds to what Cantor termed the
\textbf{Absolute Infinite} ($\Omega$)~\cite{Cantor1899}. Because the Noumena is
the `most potent source of reality', strictly un-modeled and un-limited, it
cannot be encapsulated into a single, bounded container. The set of possible
events $\mathbb{E}$ is a {\it Proper Class\/} according Von
Neumann–Bernays–Gödel (NBG) set theory~\cite{VonNeumann1925}.

As $\mathbb{E}$ is not bounded, it cannot establish a Universe as required for
the complement. There cannot be a {\tt NOT} operation on events in the
Synthetic Interstice. However, since History is by definition the {\it fixation\/}
of evolved `Nows' $N_k$, the History $H_k$ is bounded. It can act as a Universe
for Completion and any attribute of History can be processed in terms of its
{\it negation}.

In summary, we established the concept of {\it Negation\/} by means of {\it Identity}.
Further, we constraint, that the Synthetic Interstice may not operate on negated
events, but on negated attributes of History.

\section{Operations at the Synthetic Interstice}

We concluded before, that the operations of the Synthetic Interstice
necessarily exclude the {\tt NOT} operation on events in contrast to attributes
of History. With {\tt AND} as the manifestation of co-existence. Co-existence
of events is crucial for the Synthetic Interstice, otherwise there would simply
be no dynamic at all. 

The faculty of {\tt AND} establishes the faculty of {\it Composition\/} and
{\it Decomposition}. An event $C$ by $A$ {\tt AND} $B$ establishes 

\begin{equation}
     $C$ = \langle \top, \delta_C \rangle = \langle \top, \delta_A {\tt AND} \delta_B \rangle
\end{equation}

That is $\delta_C$ is the experienced as the Composition of $\delta_A$ and
$\delta_B$. On the other hand the Cognitive Subject may experience {\it
aspects\/} of $\delta_C$, namely $\delta_A$ and $\delta_B$ as separate
discernibles. {\tt OR} is established by alternative implications, i.e.

\begin{equation}
    (A \lor B) \implies C \quad \iff \quad \{ (A \implies C), (B \implies C) \} \subseteq \mathcal{I},
\end{equation}

thus, avoiding a definition with the negation of the antecedent ($A\lor B \iff
\not A \land B$ cannot be used). We are now able to define a grammar of rules
in the Synthetic Interstice using the \textbf{Backus-Naur Form
(BNF)}~\cite{Backus1959}.

\begin{formalism}[Syntax of the Synthetic Interstice]

Let $\mathcal{I}$ over the powerset of events $\mathcal{P}(\mathbb{E})$. Let
the production rule symbol be $::=$ and alternative options be $|$. The

mathematical sense. Operator {\tt WHERE} acts as a constraint condition
with the left hand value being constraint by the right hand value being
true.

    The result of the {\it Implication\/} is the existence of the event $e$ in $N_k$.

\begin{formalism}[Set of Implications $\mathcal{I}$]
    An implication $i$ in the Synthetic Interstice is a logical structure that can 
    be expressed in the form of the BNF Syntax of Implication. The set of 
    implications $\mathcal{I}$ is the set of active implications operating during 
    the Synthetic Interstice.
\end{formalism}

Each implication only adds events into existence, it never un-exists them. For
any two consecutive sets of events $N^{(i)}, N^{(i+1)} \subseteq \mathbb{E}$, it holds
that $\mathcal{I}(N^{(i)}) \subseteq \mathcal{I}(N^{(i+1)})$.

With the given BNF form of Implications, an implication becomes {\it
idempotent\/} as soon as it has caused its event. An event is unique and cannot
be removed. The History $H_k$ is immutable and therefore acts as a {\it static
constraint}, unable to induce oscillation. Consequently, with a finite set of
implications, the operations inevitably terminate. Formally, since
$\mathcal{I}$ is monotonic, Tarski's Theorem~\cite{Tarski1955} guarantees the
existence of a {\bf Least Fixed Point} regardless of cardinality.

First we treat the bounded case (local convergence).

\begin{proof}[Convergence via Monotonicity]

    Let $\mathbb{E}' \subset \mathbb{E}$ be any bounded subset of events
    relevant to the current context (e.g., the set of all possible
    implications for a finite input). Since $\mathbb{E}'$ is a Set, its
    powerset $\mathcal{P}(\mathbb{E}')$ forms a Complete Lattice. Because the
    Implication Function $\mathcal{I}$ is \textbf{Monotonic} (it preserves the
    order $\subseteq$), \textbf{Tarski's Fixed Point Theorem} guarantees that
    the sequence $N^{(i)}$ defined on $\mathbb{E}'$ converges to a Least Fixed
    Point (LFP).

    However, the Synthetic Interstice operates under strict \textbf{Positive
    Logic}. The operation is \textbf{Inflationary}:

    \begin{equation}
        N^{(k)} \subseteq N^{(k)} \cup \mathcal{I}(N^{(k)}) = N^{(k+1)}
    \end{equation}
    
    Since elements are never removed, the sequence is a \textbf{Strictly
    Ascending Chain}. An ascending chain can never oscillate; it can only:

    \begin{enumerate}

        \item \textbf{Halt:} Reach a state where $\mathcal{I}(N^{(k)})
        \subseteq N^{(k)}$ (The Fixed Point).

        \item \textbf{Transfinite Saturation:} Continue indefinitely to the
        limit ordinal $\omega$. In this case, the union of the chain
        $N^{(\infty)} = \bigcup N^{(i)}$ constitutes the Fixed Point.

    \end{enumerate}

    Therefore, even in the Absolute $\mathbb{E}$, logical oscillation is
    structurally impossible. The ``Now'' of the cognitive Subject crystallizes
    into a bounded set $N^0_k$.

\end{proof}

Consequently, the restriction of an assumed finite set of events and a finite
set of implications can be dropped. We have no reason to believe that the
Manifold with the Synthetic Interstice---being atemporal---is limited within
its capacity to operate. To the contrary, the attribute of \textit{limitedness}
appears to be intrinsic to the {\it domain of conceptualization},
rather than to the immediate interface with the un-modeled Noumena.

The {\it avoidance of circularity\/} is a necessity to avoid for the evolving
sets of events to `hang' in oscillation. The circularity is prevented though,
by exactly the property of operations on the Synthetic Interstice, as derived
before: the impossibility of a {\tt NOT} operation on events. This same
property now ensures convergence, without which perceived existence would be
impossible. 

The Synthetic Interstice in time is an \textbf{Ontological Necessity} to
separate the writing to History from the development of a fixed point. It
separates the dynamic aspect of being ($\top$) from the aspect of potential
knowing ($\bot$). The section specified a consistent grammar for the Synthetic
Interstice to establish a source of {\it becoming\/} for History.

\section{Instantiation and Implication}

Instantiations of events have their cause in the Noumena. Implications are
fixated rules. To exist, they must have been instantiated as events, which then
where fixated into History. Since, the history is immutable, those rules
themselves are immutable.

\section{Sequentiality and Enumeration}

The faculty of `writing' to history introduced a new discernible, namely by
means of the ontological truth states $\top$ vs $\bot$ of events $e = \langle
\top, \delta \rangle$ against atomic facts $a = \langle \bot, \delta \rangle$.
The differentiation of $\top$ and $\bot$, i.e.\ the differentiation of the
$\top$ in an $e_0 = \langle \top, \delta_1 \rangle$ against an atomic fact $f_1
= \langle \bot, \delta_0 \rangle$, establishes the faculty of perceiving an 
`after', and an according assertion of $\delta_1$ after $\delta_0$. 

\begin{formalism}[Sequential Fact]

    A \textbf{Sequential Fact} $s\in\mathbb{A}^\top$ is an immutable assertion
    of order between two discernibles $\delta_1$ and $\delta_0$ relating to
    an event in the Synthetic Interstice $e_0 = \langle \top, \delta_1 \rangle$ 
    and an atomic fact in History atomic fact $f_1 = \langle \bot, \delta_0 \rangle$
    defined as:
    
    \begin{equation}
        s := \langle \bot, (\delta_1 \succ \delta_0) \rangle \in \mathbb{A}^\top
    \end{equation}

    Sequentiality is established on the difference between the $\top$ in the
    events of the Synthetic Interstice and the $\bot$ in the atomic fact
    in History.

\end{formalism}

For each atomic fact $a$ in History with a $\delta_0$, the sheer existence of
an event $e$ with a $\delta_1$ in the Synthetic Interstice implies an event of
$\langle \top, \delta_1 \succ \delta_0\rangle$. Through fixation of those
events, the sequentiality of History becomes perceivable as `flow'. The
differentiation between $\top$ and $\bot$ establishes the most primitive
perception of {\it Time\/} and a dimensionality of one.

Notably, $\langle \top, \delta_1 \succ \delta_0\rangle$ is an event itself that
causes events of succession in relation to all facts in History. The set of
these implied succession events naturally converges, once all of History is
implied. But, as we saw before, even a convergence at infinity does not
contradict our framework. There is no necessity to impose History to be finite.

A special case is when an event and an atomic fact share the same discernible
$\delta$. This establishes an event of a Repetition $\langle \top, (\succ,
\delta) \rangle$ which corresponds to {\it Two-ness}. It also, established a
composition of $\succ$ and $\delta$ as a discernible $\tilde{\delta}=(\succ,
\delta)$. Now, as soon as $\delta$ appears again, another fact emerges with the same
discernible $(\succ, \delta)$. Again, through the faculty of Equivalence the
two discernibles are associated to establish a new atomic fact, namely $\langle
\bot, (\succ \succ, \delta) \rangle$ or {\it Three-ness}. 

Until the establishment of Three-ness, the composite discernible where
perceived as one $(\succ,\delta)$ to identify Equivalence. For Four-ness the
Three-ness of $\langle \bot, (\succ \succ, \delta) \rangle$ needs to be
associated with the new merged repetition $\langle \top, (\succ, \delta)
\rangle$. The faculty of Differentiation allows for the separation of the
characteristic $\delta$ out of $(\succ, \delta)$ and $(\succ \succ, \delta)$
before the perception of the equivalence in $\delta$ allows to establish
Four-ness $(\succ \succ \succ, \delta)$. With the faculty of separation of
the $\delta$ from the composite discernible and the faculty to perceive
equivalence of those discernibles, any further enumeration can be established.

\begin{formalism}[Numeric Fact]

	A \textbf{Numeric Fact} $n \in \mathbb{A}^\top$ is the immutable assertion
    of Cardinality established by recursive composition of Sequential Facts
    sharing an identical discernible $\delta$.
    
    \begin{equation}
        n := \langle \bot, (\succ^k, \delta) \rangle \in \mathbb{A}^\top
    \end{equation}

    Where $\succ^k$ represents the n-th experience of Equivalence of a
    discernable in the Synthetic Interstice with a discernable $\delta$
    in History.
\end{formalism}

The concepts of Atomic Fact $a$, Sequential Fact $s$, and Numeric Fact $n$ make
up the domain of undeniably true assertions $\mathbb{A}^\top$. Due to
ontological necessities, they are as stable as the underlying faculties of
Equivalence, Differentiation, Fixation, and Implication, on which they have
been established. They implement the concept of `stability' in contrast to the
`caducity' of events the Synthetic Interstice.

\section{Causality}

A Numeric Fact carries a specific discernible: the cardinality of the
recurrence of a Sequential Fact. While the Subject is structurally incapable of
experiencing infinity within the domain of conceptions, it possesses the
faculty to label a (magnitude of) recurrence as `Universal'. This extrapolation
constitutes the first \textbf{Refutable Assertion}---a specific fixation in
History. 

\begin{formalism}[Causal Assertion]

    A \textit{Causal Assertion} is the assertion $c \in \mathbb{A}^\diamond$ that, 
    behind a numeric fact $n \in \mathbb{A}^\top$, there exists a 
    non-conceptual reality in the Noumena that governs the regularity.

    Let $s = (\delta_{conseq} \succ \delta_{antec})$ be a Sequential Fact and $n_s$
    be the Numeric Fact representing its recurrence count. The Causal Assertion
    $c \in \mathbb{A}^\diamond$ is the speculative fixation defined as:

    \begin{equation}
        c := \langle \bot, (\varnothing, \delta_{conseq} \succ \delta_{antec}) \rangle \in \mathbb{A}^\diamond
    \end{equation}

\end{formalism}

The equation of `experienced large cardinality' to `infinity' and the assertion
of an existence of a separable `reality of a causal nexus' in the
un-conceptualizable Absolute (in which no multitude or separation can exist)
establishes the concept of Wrongness $\varnothing$---a discernible that a Causal
Assertion carries along. 

Causal Assertions have significant implications on the mode of experience of
the cognitive Subject. It is the first concept that emerges from the necessity
of limitedness of the conceptualization, not from the primordial concepts of
Equivalence, Differentiation, Implication and Fixation. And it is the first
exposure to an essence of Wrongness. The cardinality of its underlying Numeric
Fact, however, provides grades of stability, i.e.\ {\it grades of truth\/} and the
concept of ``{\it More-is-More-True\/}''. 

The $\varnothing$ discernible is the primal experience of Non-Truth. When
ignored, it carries the essence of {\it Illusion\/}: the belief that a
`causal reason'---which is a concept---exists, while the all conceptual experience
emerges through the Synthetic Interstice triggered by events which are
instantiated from the unconceivable Noumena. When the $\varnothing$ discernible
is ignored, the Cognitive Subject may actually live in the illusionary world of
conceptualization. 

\section{Topology}

Since the faculty of Equivalence and Differentiation is already established in
the Synthetic Interstice, Implications can establish events that represent
`adjacency' between events like succession does in the sequence of History.
Topology can now be established as a `causality' orthogonal to primitive time.

\begin{formalism}[Topological Event]

    A \textbf{Topological Event} $e_t \in \mathbb{E}$ is an ephemeral entity
    emerging within the Synthetic Interstice that manifests the mutual
    implication between two co-existing events.

    Let $\bowtie$ denote the adjacency operator (representing structural
    ``closeness''). If two distinct events $e_1 = \langle \top, \delta_1
    \rangle$ and $e_2 = \langle \top, \delta_2 \rangle$ imply each other via
    the Implication Set $\mathcal{I}$ during the interstice:

    \begin{equation}
        (e_1 \implies e_2) \quad \land \quad (e_2 \implies e_1)
    \end{equation}

    then an implication event $e_t$ is generated:

    \begin{equation}
        e_t := \langle \top, (\delta_1 \bowtie \delta_2) \rangle
    \end{equation}

    The event $e_t$ exists purely in the domain of Becoming ($\top$) and
    carries the composite discernible $(\delta_1 \bowtie \delta_2)$. It serves as
    the fundamental unit of dynamic topological extension prior to any historical fixation.

\end{formalism}

\begin{formalism}[Topological Assertion]

    A \textbf{Topological Assertion} $t \in \mathbb{A}^\diamond$ is the speculative fixation
    that, behind a recurring network of Topological Facts $p \in \mathbb{A}^\top$, there
    exists a non-conceptual reality in the Noumena that governs this synchronous regularity.

    Let $p = (\delta_1 \bowtie \delta_2)$ be a Topological Fact. The Topological Assertion
    $t \in \mathbb{A}^\diamond$ posits that this adjacency is an intrinsic rule of the
    metaphysical substance, defined as:

    \begin{equation}
        t := (p \xrightarrow{\mathcal{N}} \top)
    \end{equation}

    Like the Causal Assertion, it is refutable; it remains valid only as long as the
    Synthetic Interstice continues to reliably produce the adjacency $p$ in future states.

\end{formalism}
