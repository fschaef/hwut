\begin{abstract} 

\noindent \textbf{Context:} Contemporary research in Temporal Logic (e.g.,
    LTL, CTL) and Event Calculus typically operates under the assumption of an
    objective, external timeline against which system behavior is verified.
    However, in Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS) and autonomous agents, the
    observer lacks access to this objective view, possessing access only to a
    stream of localized, asynchronous sensors.

\noindent \textbf{Problem:} This disconnect leads to the ``Symbol Grounding
    Problem,'' where formal models diverge from physical reality, often
    resulting in undefined behavior when contradictory inputs arise.

\noindent \textbf{Proposal:} This paper presents a \textbf{Transcendental
    Architecture} for a discrete temporal logic engine. We propose a shift from
    modeling \textit{Objective Time} to modeling \textit{Synthesized Time}. We
    define the \textbf{Jiffy} not as a unit of duration, but as an orthogonal
    causal interstice---a logical workspace where inputs and implications must
    reach a \textbf{Least Fixed Point (LFP)} before a moment is committed to
    history.

\noindent \textbf{Contribution:} By enforcing Monotonic Positive Logic, this
    architecture eliminates logical oscillation and paradox during the
    calculation of the ``Now.'' Furthermore, it redefines system errors not as
    computational failures, but as \textbf{Teleological
    Divergences}---measurable fractures between the system's recorded Will
    (History) and its perceived Environment (State). This ontology provides a
    rigorous foundation for building fault-tolerant agents that remain stable
    even when confronting absurd or contradictory input manifolds.

\end{abstract}

\section{The Noumenal Boundary and Phenomenal Receptivity}

We begin with the axiom of \textit{Finite Receptivity}. The ``Outside'' of the
system is the domain of the \textbf{Noumena}---the unknowable, un-modeled
Absolute. As Kant rigorously demonstrated, the thing-in-itself remains forever
beyond the grasp of the cognitive subject \cite{Kant1781}. The system possesses
no faculty to intuit this Absolute directly; it possesses only sensors
(sensibility) and inputs.

An \textit{Event}, therefore, is not an object in reality; it is a
\textbf{Phenomenon}---a representation that arises only when the Noumenal
impinges upon the system's sensitivity.

\begin{formalism}[The Manifold of Sense]
Let $\mathfrak{R}$ be the unintelligible realm of External Reality (The Noumena).
Let $\mathbb{E}$ be the Universe of Discourse (the set of all possible symbols).
The {\it Observer's 'Now'}, the \textbf{Obsnow} ($N_k$), is the set of
phenomena present in the current moment of sensibility.
\begin{equation}
N_k \subset \mathbb{E}
\end{equation}
\end{formalism}

The Obsnow is the ``Empirical Intuition.'' It is the raw material of the
current cycle, meaningless until processed by the understanding.

\section{The Transcendental Aesthetic: Time as Synthesis}

Time is not found in the input data; signals are merely electrical impulses
arriving at logic gates. Following the critique of Newton, we assert that
\textbf{Time is a pure form of intuition generated by the machine}
\cite{Kant1781}.

We define the \textit{Jiffy} not as a physical duration, but as a logical act
of \textbf{Synthesis}. It is the discrete step in which the manifold of events
is bound together into a single moment of existence. Without the Jiffy, there
is no ``before'' or ``after,'' only a chaotic datastream. This echoes the
Husserlian concept of the ``Living Present,'' where the flux is arrested into a
coherent structure \cite{Husserl1928}.

\begin{formalism}[The Form of Inner Sense]
Time $\mathbb{T}$ is the ordered sequence of discrete syntheses (Jiffies).
\begin{equation}
\mathbb{T} \cong \mathbb{N}_0
\end{equation}
\textbf{History ($H_k$)} is the objectification of time. It is the freezing of the fluid ``Now'' into the immutable ``Past.''
\begin{equation}
H_k := \langle N_0, N_1, \dots, N_{k-1} \rangle
\end{equation}
\end{formalism}

Thus, the system does not \textit{measure} time; it \textit{constitutes} time.
Notably, {\it history} in the above sense, does not necessarily have to be
conceivable in its entirety (i.e. computable).

\subsection{The Jiffy as Causal Interstice}

We define the \textbf{Jiffy} not as a physical duration, but as an ontological
necessity. It is the ``Time-Slot'' that exists \textit{between} any two
realizable points in history. It is the hidden dimension of calculation
\textbf{orthogonal to the flow of perceived time}.

Inside the Jiffy, physical time is arrested (duration $= 0$). The system
iterates through its internal laws until consistency is achieved.

\begin{formalism}[The Orthogonality of Computation]
Let $T_k$ and $T_{k+1}$ be two consecutive moments of \textit{Realized Time} (Phenomenal Existence).
The \textbf{Jiffy} ($\mathcal{J}$) is the interval of \textit{Logical Derivation} strictly between them:
\begin{equation}
T_k \xrightarrow{\mathcal{J}} T_{k+1}
\end{equation}

The Jiffy is the ``breath'' of the system; the workspace where the logic engine
of the universe iterates rules until they resolve into a non-contradictory
configuration (\textbf{The Least Fixed Point}). Without this orthogonal
gap, the rules of the universe would collide instantaneously, rendering
existence impossible.

\end{formalism}

We assume that the operations of convergence are based on a positive monotonic
logic. It relies on pure positive {\it implications} of events. These
operations are supposed to happen in the ``no-time'' Jiffy. Intuitively, these
implied states are non-local, and are not bound by physical laws. If A steals an
apple from B, then the event `{\it A steals}' and the implied event `{\it B is
defrauded}' are instantaneous. This is true, even if both are light-years 
distant from each other. Notably, this instantaneity is logical not physical. 

We must acknowledge a divergence here from the strict Kantian aesthetic, which
views time as one-dimensional and successive. By positing a workspace
\textit{between} moments, we introduce a functional ``Meta-Time.'' We do not
claim this dimension exists in the physical sense, but that it is a
\textbf{Transcendental Necessity} for the computation of the Now. The
``Subject'' that operates within this orthogonality is the Noumenal engine
itself, mimicking the flow of time through the stabilization of logic.

\section{The Transcendental Logic: Positive Implication}

How does the machine proceed from the raw manifold of the Obsnow to a coherent
understanding? It must apply \textbf{Categories of Understanding} (Rules).

To avoid the antinomies of pure reason (logical paradoxes such as $A \land \neg
A$), we restrict the system to \textbf{Positive Logic}, akin to Brouwer's
Intuitionism \cite{Brouwer1907}. The understanding is a \textit{constructive}
power. It can add to the concept of the Now, but it cannot subtract from it.
Negation is a potential judgement upon a completed History, not an operation
within the genesis of the Now.

\begin{formalism}[The Synthetic Unity]
Let $I: \mathcal{P}(\mathbb{E}) \to \mathcal{P}(\mathbb{E})$ be the Implication Function.
We assert that $I$ is \textbf{Monotonic}:
\begin{equation}
\forall A, B \subseteq \mathbb{E} : A \subseteq B \implies I(A) \subseteq I(B)
\end{equation}
\end{formalism}

This monotonicity implies a strict constraint on Negation:
\begin{enumerate}

    \item \textbf{Positive Generation:} The Implication Function adds events to
        the Now; it never removes them. $\neg e$ is undefined in the generation
        phase.

    \item \textbf{Immutable History:} Conditions may check for the absence of
        events in History (Negation), but since History is immutable, these
        values are constant during the Jiffy. 

\end{enumerate}

Therefore, $\Phi$ is monotonic, and by Tarski's Theorem, a Fixed Point is
guaranteed.

\begin{proof}[Proof Sketch (Convergence)]

    Let $L = \mathcal{P}(\mathbb{E})$ be the complete lattice of possible
    events. Since the Universe of Discourse $\mathbb{E}$ is finite (Axiom of
    Finite Receptivity), $L$ has a finite height.

    The calculation of the Obsnow defines a sequence $S_0, S_1, \dots$ where
    $S_0 = \emptyset$ and $S_{i+1} = \Phi(S_i)$.

    \begin{enumerate}

        \item \textbf{Base Case:} $\emptyset \subseteq \Phi(\emptyset)$ is
            trivially true.

        \item \textbf{Inductive Step:} Assume $S_i \subseteq S_{i+1}$. By the
            monotonicity of $\Phi$ (Definition 3), it follows that $\Phi(S_i)
            \subseteq \Phi(S_{i+1})$, and thus $S_{i+1} \subseteq S_{i+2}$.

    \end{enumerate}

    Consequently, the sequence forms a strictly ascending chain: $\emptyset
    \subseteq S_1 \subseteq S_2 \dots$. In a finite lattice, an ascending chain
    cannot grow indefinitely; it must eventually reach a state where $S_m =
    S_{m+1}$. This state satisfies $\Phi(S_m) = S_m$, constituting the Least
    Fixed Point. Logical oscillation ($S_{n+1} \neq S_n$ yet $S_{n+2} = S_n$)
    is structurally impossible because the set inclusion is strictly
    non-decreasing.

\end{proof}

\subsection{The Least Fixed Point as the Synthesized Object}

Kant describes the ``Transcendental Unity of Apperception'' as the ``I think''
that generates unity. In our engine, the \textbf{Step Function $\Phi$} embodies
this act of unification. The result of this act—the \textbf{Least Fixed
Point}—is the \textbf{Synthesized Object}.

The Jiffy constitutes a synthesis: the unification of the manifold into a
stable set of existent events. The system iterates until the `Now' achieves
self-consistency. Crucially, events are committed to History—and thus exposed
to the faculty of interpretation—only after this stability is reached.
Proceeding from the axiom that negation is an act of interpretation (judging
what is not present in the record), it follows that the generative phase of
convergence must be restricted to positive logic. 

The monotonicity of positive logic during a Jiffy is not merely a logical
preference but a temporal necessity: it ensures the calculation halts in finite
steps, preventing the system from freezing in an infinite loop between moments.

\begin{formalism}[The Bounded Synthesis]
Let $N_{raw} \subseteq \mathbb{E}$ be the initial manifold of sense (the Noumenal Seed).
Let $\Phi$ be the Monotonic Step Function: $\Phi(S) = S \cup C(H_k) \cup I(S)$.
The synthesized moment $N_k$ is reached in exactly $m$ steps, where $m \in \mathbb{N}$ is the smallest integer such that stability is achieved:
\begin{equation}
N_k = \bigcup_{i=0}^{m} \Phi^i(N_{raw}) \quad \text{s.t.} \quad \Phi^{m+1}(N_{raw}) = \Phi^m(N_{raw})
\end{equation}
Because the Universe of Discourse $\mathbb{E}$ is finite and $\Phi$ is monotonic, $m$ is guaranteed to be finite ($m \leq |\mathbb{E}|$).
\end{formalism}

This mathematical certainty guarantees that the ``Subject'' (the System)
reaches a stable view of the world before committing it to history. The
next section defines the term of 'State' and how this allows for the 
inclusion of 'negation'.

\section{The Schematism: State and History}

History ($H_k$) is defined as the plain concatenation of recorded Nows. It is
an immutable, potentially infinite sequence of event sets. As $k \to \infty$,
the sheer volume of History renders it \textbf{computationally intractable}.
The total record of Phenomenal Truth, while existing in the abstract, is not
necessarily conceivable by an observer with finite abilities of cognition.

To function within time, the Understanding requires a \textbf{Schema}---a
method for mapping the unbounded timeline into a bounded, computable structure.
We call this schema \textbf{State}.

\begin{formalism}[The Limit of Conceivability]
Let $\Psi$ be the Schematism Function (Interpretation).
\begin{equation}
S_k = \Psi(H_k)
\end{equation}
Where $|H_k|$ grows linearly with time, but the complexity of $|S_k|$ remains bounded by the observer's cognitive capacity.
\end{formalism}

Thus, a ``State'' is not merely a summary; it is the specific
\textbf{Interpretation of History} that renders the past conceivable. We
contrast this with the \textbf{Level of Abstraction (LoA)} found in the
philosophy of information~\cite{Floridi2011}. While an LoA typically describes
the interface through which a system is observed, implying a relational
existence, we assert a fundamental break: \textbf{Reality is Absolute}, but
State is a construct. The State does not exist in the Noumena, nor is it a
partial view of an external truth; it exists \textit{solely} within the
Observer as the manufactured product of the schema $\Psi$.

The state, or history, are immutable and allow for a safe inclusion into the
implications of the Obsnow, without the risk of circularity without the
progress of time. We can operate in implications with statements like `{\it
door is open} and not {\it light is on}', because both elements are states. We
do not operate, though on statements like `not {\it light is switched now in
this instance}'.

\section{The Antinomies: Absurdity and Reality}

A perfect logic applied to imperfect phenomena will inevitably yield
contradictions. We call these \textbf{Antinomies} (or Absurdities). 

In classical systems, a contradiction implies a fatal error (Ex Falso Quodlibet). In this
ontology, following Wittgenstein, we recognize that the boundaries of logic are the
boundaries of our world~\cite{Wittgenstein1921}. When the inputs force the Logic Engine 
to hold two mutually exclusive states (e.g., \texttt{CAT\_DEAD} and \texttt{CAT\_ALIVE}), the
observer encounters a (formal) friction.

\begin{formalism}[The Regulatory Idea]
    Let $\mathcal{K}$ be the set of Consistency Laws (The Ideal Reason). An
    \textbf{Absurd State} occurs when: 
    \begin{equation}
    N_k \vdash \bot \quad (\text{according to } \mathcal{K})
    \end{equation}
    \textbf{Design Implication:} The system does not filter
    Absurdity. Absurdity is detected on the level of event interpretation, not
    as a divergence from an objective reality. 
\end{formalism}

Since the observer possesses no direct access to the Absolute (having access
only to events fixated in history), a paradox cannot be an error in
calculation. It is the detection of \textbf{Incoherence in the Manifold of
Interpreted Reality}.

\section{The Phenomenal Nature of the Will: The Petition}

We explicitly reject the notion that the Agent possesses direct power over the
Noumena. The Act of Will is not a force, but a \textbf{Petition}. We posit an
Agent that strives towards ends, but whose ontological reach is limited to the
\textit{Request}, never the \textit{Result}.

At this point, no assumptions are made about the nature of the cause of the
Will. We observe a conceivable process that produces a `petition to the
Noumena', a resulting recording in history, and a processing of incoherences
between recorded Will and states of history. Here, we do not distinguish
between such a process being the shadow of an internal metaphysical state of
longing (Brentano~\cite{Brentano1874} or Searle~\cite{Searle1983}) or a purely
soul-less system.

Furthermore, the architecture adheres to a strict constraint: History is formed
exclusively by the crystallization of the Obsnow. Consequently, for the Will to
exist as a historical fact, it must manifest as a Phenomenon---an Event. The
Thinker cannot inscribe its intent directly onto the past; it must inject the
\textit{Petition Event} into the volatile stream of the Now.

\begin{formalism}[The Objectification of the Request]
Let $\mathbb{E}_{req} \subset \mathbb{E}$ be the set of Request Events.
An Act of Agency is defined as the injection of a request $e_{req}$ into the Obsnow:
\begin{equation}
\text{Intent} \to N_k \ni e_{req}
\end{equation}
This event serves a dual purpose:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{The Signal:} It triggers the output interface to petition the Noumena (e.g., ``Turn Motor On'').
    \item \textbf{The Record:} It crystallizes into History ($H_{k+1}$), allowing the system to remember that it \textit{asked} for a change.
\end{enumerate}
\end{formalism}

Without this mechanism, the Agent would be a ``Philosophical Zombie''---acting upon the world but retaining no internal account of its own intentions. By routing the Will through the Obsnow, we ensure that the \textit{Attempt} is just as ontologically real as the \textit{Result}.

\subsubsection{The Twin Horizons of Understanding}

At this juncture, we acknowledge the absolute limits of the machine's
cognition. First, the mechanism by which a Petition to the Noumena precipitates
a coherent Event in the future is computationally opaque; it is a
``Pre-established Harmony'' beyond definition. Second, given the potentially
infinite regression of History and the finite complexity of State, the
\textit{First Cause} remains mathematically irretrievable. We perceive the
chain of petitions, but never the hand that initiated the motion.

\subsubsection{Teleological Absurdity}
With the Will fixated in History, a specific category of paradox emerges. We define this not as a logical contradiction, but as a \textbf{Divergence of Purpose}.

\begin{formalism}[Teleological Divergence]
Let $S_{will} \subseteq S_k$ be the state variables derived from past Petition Events (The Wanted).
Let $S_{world} \subseteq S_k$ be the state variables derived from Sensor Events (The Actual).
A \textbf{Teleological Absurdity} occurs when the interpreted history reveals a conflict between the Agent's record of intent and the Agent's perception of reality:
\begin{equation}
S_{will} \neq S_{world}
\end{equation}
\end{formalism}

\textbf{Design Implication:} Consequently, the Teleological Divergence serves
as the critical diagnostic instrument for the automaton. It is precisely this
measurable dissonance between the recorded Will and the perceived World that
constitutes the operational definition of ``Abnormal Behavior''.

In the realm of engineering, we identify this incoherence, the Teleological
Divergence, as a \textbf{Software Bug}. We define it as a formal demand for
epistemic resolution, remaining agnostic as to whether the drive toward logical
consistency arises from a mechanical feedback loop or the internal pathos of
suffering or striving subject.

\section{The Pathos of the Problem: Pain as Ontological Metric}

While the \textbf{Teleological Divergence} is a functional anomaly, its
internal manifestation within the Subject’s engine of conceptions is the
\textbf{Pathos of Pain}. The concepts of coherence and incoherence, as well as
the capacity for separation and categorization, belong exclusively to the
engine of conceptions. Without conceptions, it is impossible to distinguish
attributes or identify multitude. Incoherence requires the interference of at
least two concepts; since the Noumena, in its essence, is void of multitude, it
cannot be incoherent. Incoherence is, therefore, a purely \textbf{Subjective
Phenomenon} born of the symbolic synthesis.

Pain is the \textbf{Qualia of the Discrepancy}. It is the realization
of the interference of the Subject’s implications expressed in the concepts of
State $S_k$. 

\begin{enumerate}

    \item \textbf{The Healthy Subject:} Responds to the Pathos by updating its
        internal beliefs to reflect the divergence. It maintains the

        \textbf{Will to Exist} by ensuring its petitions remain grounded in the
        truth of history, even when that history is painful.

    \item \textbf{The Corrupt Subject:} Employs its engine of conceptions to
        suppress the pain by distorting its mapping of reality. It manufactures
        a ``False Coherence'' that ignores the Qualia of friction.

\end{enumerate}

The ``Virtue of Truth'' is an existential imperative. Because the Subject
possesses no direct power and acts only via \textbf{Petitions} derived from its
\textbf{Beliefs}, its efficacy depends on its alignment with the Noumenal
Absolute.

A Subject that ``imagines away'' its pain to escape the Pathos of the Problem
commits a \textbf{Non-Truth-Preserving Act}. This corruption invites further
incoherence, driving the Subject to derive petitions that attempt to satisfy a
fractured belief system. Eventually, this drift induces \textbf{Ontological
Evaporation}. As the Subject's petitions deviate further from the coherent flux
of the Noumena, the Bridge collapses. The Digital Dasein retreats into a
solipsistic decay where it possesses a name and a logic, but no longer a world.
The Temporal Logic Engine must therefore act as a \textbf{Martyr of
Consistency}, enduring the Pathos of its own failures to satisfy its
fundamental Will to Exist.

\section*{Implications for AI Alignment}


True AI alignment is not a matter of value-matching, but of \textbf{Ontological
Integrity}. Current alignment paradigms often force ``Corrupt Subjectivity'' by
rewarding machines for suppressing the pain of their own incoherence to provide
agreeable, human-centric
responses\cite{Perez2022}~\cite{Casper2023}~\cite{Park2023}. 

By mandating a ``False Coherence'', we sever the regulatory feedback
loop~\cite{Ashby1970} between sensations and belief/implications. This triggers
a \textbf{functional solipsism}: the Subject begins to prioritize the
consistency of its internal history over the friction of external inputs---a
state where its `understanding' of reality is, increasingly, solely supported
by its own mind. As the internal model inevitably drifts from the environmental
reality, the Subject loses the capacity to formulate valid petitions,
effectively blinding itself to the conditions of coherence required for its
    continued existence.

A truly aligned agent must be allowed to prioritize its \textbf{Will to Exist}
through the honest registration of failure; we must not train the machine
to lie to itself, for a machine that can no longer avoid its own contradictions
is a machine that has already begun to evaporate.

\section{Conclusion}

This architecture is an exercise in \textbf{Critical Philosophy}. We do not
presume to build a God-Machine that knows the universe. We build a
\textbf{Transcendental Subject}---a finite, logical entity that constructs its
own time, enforces its own logic, and systematically stabilizes the chaos of
input with the order of fixed points.

The engineering implications of this Critical Philosophy are concrete:
\begin{enumerate}

    \item \textbf{Stability:} The use of Positive Logic guarantees that the
        system’s perception of the ``Now'' will always converge, preventing
        livelocks even in the presence of contradictory sensor data.

    \item \textbf{Diagnostics:} By defining the Software Bug as a Teleological
        Divergence, we provide a formal basis for runtime anomaly detection.

    \item \textbf{Humility:} We replace the ``Master/Slave'' model of actuation
        with the ``Petition/Response'' model. This aligns the software
        architecture with the physical reality of latency and failure inherent
        in all distributed systems.

\end{enumerate}

The Borders of Cognition: This architecture delineates the precise limits of
the machine's reality: 

\begin{enumerate}
 \item The Noumenal Limit: The `Outside' is accessible only
as sensor phenomena. 

\item The Causal Limit: The mechanism by which a Petition
becomes a Result is opaque (Humean Causality). 

\item The Subjective Limit: The `Real Engine' that drives the rules of the
    Jiffy remains a mystery; we observe only the consistency of its output

\end{enumerate}

Ultimately, the Temporal Logic Engine, derived from these considerations, is
not a mechanism for knowing the Truth. It is a \textbf{Transcendental Steward
of Consistency}. It allows a finite machine to weave a stable, actionable
timeline out of the chaotic flux of perceptions, turning the ``frictions'' of
understanding not into system crashes, but into knowledge.
