===============================================================================
disc-9  LITERAL LEAVES CARRY THE ARM ROLE HINT (drop the _const(ENUM) table)
===============================================================================

STATUS: open choice. Sketched, not built. Removes "Blocker 1" from the parser
        map (see disc-10). Same principle as A-12: the parser carries the
        uninterpreted typed token; meaning is assigned in pass 2.

THE THING
    Today the literal arms of the atom/number OR rules route through a per-kind
    factory that STAMPS an application enum onto the leaf:

        "algebr/atom": OrMap({(...): PASS,
                              "opaque": OpaqueLeaf.from_span,
                              "string": _const(E_ConstantKind.STRING),
                              ("true","false"): _const(E_ConstantKind.BOOL)}),
        "number":      OrMap({"float": _const(E_ConstantKind.FLOAT),
                              "int":   _const(E_ConstantKind.INT)}),

    _const(KIND) is a terminal->enum TRANSLATION TABLE living in the parser. It
    is the only non-shape input in those arms: the grammar knows WHICH literal
    terminal fired (the arm's role, already tagged) but not what value-DOMAIN it
    denotes; _const supplies the domain. That domain assignment is meaning, not
    shape -- it does not belong in the parser.

THE MOVE
    The fired OR node already exposes or_node.role (the arm tag) and
    or_node.child (the matched terminal, with .text/.begin). Build the leaf
    carrying the ROLE HINT as its 'kind', with NO enum translation:

        # ConstantLeaf.from_token (CST-shape-aware wrapper; reads the OR node)
        tok = or_node.child
        return cls(text=tok.text, kind=or_node.role, begin=tok.begin)
        # kind is 'string' | 'true' | 'false' | 'int' | 'float'

    The map arms then collapse to ONE uniform factory:

        "algebr/atom": OrMap({(...): PASS,
                              "opaque": OpaqueLeaf.from_span,
                              ("string","true","false"): ConstantLeaf.from_token}),
        "number":      ConstantLeaf.from_token   # both arms -> same factory;
                                                 # 'number' no longer needs OrMap

WHY IT IS RIGHT
    ConstantLeaf was already designed for this: core/symbol/ast.py frames 'kind'
    as "the lexical kind it was born from ... the application is the only party
    that interprets" it. A role string honours that more truthfully than a
    pre-translated enum. The hint comes FREE from the role tag the grammar wrote;
    carrying it is just not discarding information the parser already has.

WHERE THE TABLE GOES (it does not vanish; it relocates to where it belongs)
    The hint->domain collapse -- crucially {true,false}->BOOL, two terminals one
    domain -- moves to the semantic layer (F-5), consumed when const-fold needs a
    domain:

        _DOMAIN = {"string": E_ConstantKind.STRING, "int": ...INT,
                   "float": ...FLOAT, "true": ...BOOL, "false": ...BOOL}

    E_ConstantKind stops being a PARSER product and becomes the SEMANTIC layer's
    domain enum (the codomain of _DOMAIN). It moves with the table.

SAFE TODAY
    ConstantLeaf.kind is WRITE-ONLY across the whole tree right now: four writers
    (the _const arms), zero readers (verified). Switching the field's contents
    from enum to hint-string breaks nothing until pass-2 const-fold is built, at
    which point that pass reads the hint and applies _DOMAIN as its first step.

OPEN SEAMS (settle before building)
  S1  Role becomes CONTRACTUAL, not advisory. Once kind IS the role string, the
      arm tags ("string"/"true"/...) are load-bearing: pass 2 keys on them, so
      renaming an arm silently changes a leaf's kind. The OrMap docstring calls
      role "advisory"; for these arms it would become a contract. This slightly
      bends "roles classify, positions address" -- here a role also carries data
      forward. Record the exception explicitly if adopted.
  S2  Placement of from_token. It must read or_node.role, so it knows about CST
      OR nodes -> it lives on the PARSER's ConstantLeaf view (alongside the
      from_seq/from_or family), NOT in core/symbol (which must stay ignorant of
      OR nodes). from_text(text,kind,begin) stays the general core primitive;
      from_token(or_node) is the parser-side shape-aware wrapper over it.

EXPECTED COST WHEN BUILT
    GOOD diff is structural-only: leaf reprs change from kind=E_ConstantKind.STRING
    to kind='string'. One attributable change; regenerate, triage value-exact.
