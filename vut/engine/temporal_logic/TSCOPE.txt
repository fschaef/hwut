A Guard is a Scope Template. It is not a singleton; the engine spawns multiple Instances of a scope based on incoming events.

Trigger (The Anchor):

Syntax: on EVENT(.id => ID)

The Anchoring Rule: A Scope activation condition MUST contain at least one External Event (from the stream) as an anchor.

Prohibition: A Scope cannot be activated solely by internal events (Action-generated) or History queries. This prevents "runaway" self-sustaining loops.


Instantiation:

When the on condition is met, the Engine creates a new Scope Instance.

The Instance "captures" the bound variables (e.g., ID=101) and isolates them
from other instances.

Since every Scope requires an external anchor (or an anchor derived from a
finite external sequence), and internal events cannot sustain themselves
indefinitely without new external input, the chain must terminate
